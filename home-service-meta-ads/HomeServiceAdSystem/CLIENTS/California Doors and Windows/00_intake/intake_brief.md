## 5. MARKET CONTEXT

### Climate (San Diego coastal Southern California)
- **Salt air corrosion** is a documented driver in San Diego coastal homes. Salt particles settle on frames and hardware; moisture accelerates corrosion and seal breakdown. Aluminum frames pit; wood frames swell/contract; vinyl can become brittle. **Vinyl and fiberglass** are the recommended frame materials for coastal exposure. [^climate1][^climate2]
- **Year-round high-UV sun** exposure breaks down sealants and finishes faster than in inland markets. [^climate3]
- **Marine layer** humidity drives more frequent maintenance windows than inland CA. [^climate1]
- **Seasonal demand pattern: UNVERIFIED.** Search did not return a clean San Diego window-replacement seasonality study. Industry intuition (national): Q2-Q3 peak (warm-weather projects), winter dips. Confirm with client's own historical lead data before targeting decisions.

### Demographics — primary service area (San Diego County)
- **ZIP 92109 (Pacific Beach)** — the primary listed business address area:
  - Population: 44,671 residents in 22,031 households.
  - Median age: 33.7 (younger than CA state median 37.5 and national 38.8) — note: this skews toward renters and condo dwellers, not the typical homeowner buyer. Window replacement buyers will be the older/owner subset of the broader county.
  - Median household income: $122,128 (well above national median $80,734).
  - Race/ethnicity: 77.1% White, 13.9% Hispanic/Latino. [^demo_92109]
- **Pacific Beach housing market:**
  - Typical home value (Zillow, ZIP 92109): $1,351,349.

  - YTD median sale price for condo/townhome: $895,000.
  - Pacific Beach-specific (Redfin, Mar 2026): median sold $1.5M, +14.8% YoY. [^demo_91029_zillow][^demo_redfin]

### Building codes & permits (San Diego County)
- **Title 24 (CA Building Energy Efficiency Standards):** all replacement windows must have U-factor ≤0.30, SHGC ≤0.23, double-pane minimum, Low-E2 coating — **statewide** for projects replacing >75 sq ft of glazing. For <75 sq ft, U ≤0.40 and SHGC ≤0.35 acceptable. [^title24_1][^title24_milgard]
- **City of San Diego permits:** required when window replacement changes opening size or framing. Like-for-like replacements (same size/style/location, no framing change) often do NOT require a permit.
