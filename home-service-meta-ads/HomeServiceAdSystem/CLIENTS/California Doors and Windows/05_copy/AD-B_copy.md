## 1% FLEX NOTES

- **Headline line 2 word swap: "WITH OUR SPRING SPECIAL" → "— LIMITED TIME"** is a 1% flex used here. Justifications:
  1. **Seasonal language dates the ad.** "SPRING SPECIAL" times the creative to a calendar season; if the ad runs into summer or fall it reads stale, which would force a re-cut on a structurally fine clone. "LIMITED TIME" carries the same urgency without dating the creative.
  2. **Pairs with the locked vague-terms compliance language.** CDW's Round 1 offer disclaimer is "Limited-time offer — see in-home estimate for details" (asset audit Section 2 lock). The headline's "LIMITED TIME" mirrors the compliance line, which keeps the CYA disclosure visible at two reading heights (headline and fine print).
  3. **Word count parity.** Reference line 2 has 7 words; the swap has 6 words — within the ±1–2 word 1% flex budget.
  4. **Direct-response energy preserved.** Question on line 1 + offer/urgency answer on line 2; black/red ALL CAPS rhythm; same line count. The reference's structural mechanic is intact.
- All other elements clone REF-002 at 99% with no further flex.
