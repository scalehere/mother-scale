## 2. WHICH AWARENESS STAGE HAS THE HIGHEST LEVERAGE FOR THIS CLIENT?

**Primary stage: SOLUTION AWARE.**

Three reasons.

**Proof stack fires hardest here.** CDW's defensible proof — Mike Marohnic personally on every coastal job, 35 years same-owner, CSLB #537570 verifiable since 1988, Best of Houzz 2026, marine-grade coastal mechanism, 13 verbatim "no-pressure / easy to work with / fair" reviews — is built precisely for the moment a buyer is comparing three quotes and asking "who do I trust?" That moment lives in Solution Aware. Problem Aware needs education; Most Aware needs offer. Solution Aware needs *trust transfer*, which is the only thing CDW has more of than every chain in the market.

**Competitors are most exploitable here.** The chain playbook (today-only, premium experience, lifetime warranty, starting-from pricing, kitchen-table close) all happens at Solution Aware — and all five tropes are flagged in the Bible as L4–5 distrust triggers. CDW's anti-chain wedge inverts every one of them in the same scroll: "No commission rep. No 'today only.' Mike measures, Mike quotes, Mike installs. Same phone number since 1988." The chains literally cannot say this without being sued. That's an irreplicable structural moat at the exact stage where the buyer is most ready to choose.

**Volume × intent × CPA economics.** Problem Aware has 5 percentage points more volume but requires 2–3x more touches to convert on a $14K–$22K considered purchase. With capacity-per-day still UNVERIFIED (Intake §6) and the photo asset library reduced to one Mike portrait + AI-generated process imagery for round 1, the campaign cannot afford to spend Round 1 dollars buying education impressions. Solution Aware buyers are 30–60 days from purchase, already self-identified as window/door shoppers (search + interest signals), and convert on trust — which is exactly what the asset stack supports. CPA will be lower, attribution faster, and the data we get back from this stage will tell us whether to expand to Problem Aware in Round 2.

The Bible recommended a 40/30/20/10 budget weighting toward Problem Aware. That recommendation optimizes for *long-term* lead-pool development. Round 1 needs proof-of-creative and proof-of-economics — both of which Solution Aware delivers faster.

## 6. THE COMPETITIVE GAP MAP

### Saturated lanes — what competitors are saying. CDW does NOT say these.

1. **"Today only" / "limited-time price."** Every chain commission rep closes with this. The Bible flags it three places (Hooks #12, #38, #95) as a chain tell that triggers L4 distrust. CDW's standing rule: no time-bound urgency that isn't truthfully verifiable in writing.
2. **"Premium experience" / "premium installation."** Bible Hook #40 verbatim: *"If a window contractor uses the words 'premium experience' — close the door."* Saturated by RbA and the regional Pella dealers. Banned word per Project Instructions.
3. **"Lifetime warranty"** without specifics. Saturated by every national chain. Bible Hooks #62, #80, #98 flag this as franchise marketing. CDW will not use the bare phrase; warranty language only with verbatim terms once Mike confirms.
4. **"Starting from $X"** / "from $XXX per window." Bait-and-switch tell at Sheet 4 L3. Saturated across Window World, American Vision, Champion. CDW will not lead with a "from" price.
5. **"Energy efficient" / "save on your energy bills"** without numbers. Generic claim used by everyone; meaningless to L4 buyers. CDW will only claim energy efficiency with U-factor/SHGC numbers attached.

### Open lanes — what no competitor is saying. CDW CAN say these. *This is the creative wedge.*

1. **"Mike measures, Mike quotes, Mike installs. Owner answers the warranty call."** Single-founder accountability across the full project lifecycle. No chain can credibly say this — the chain model is built on commission reps, subcontracted install crews, and 1-800 warranty queues. Direct contradiction of the chain pattern, structurally irreplicable.
2. **"Same phone number since 1988. (888) 804-7775. Ask Mike."** Verifiable, irreplicable, anchors 35-year tenure in a single concrete proof. Chains rotate phone numbers per market and per franchise transfer; CDW's number is the same number that picked up in 1988.
3. **"No deposit until you've signed the contract AND seen the spec sheet."** Direct inverse of the chain kitchen-table close (deposit-to-hold-the-quote). Documented in Bible Sheet 4 L5 + Hook #28 + Objection 4. Removes the single largest first-call objection.
4. **"One crew, one invoice — windows, doors, dry rot, stucco patch, interior finish."** Multi-trade scope (per Intake §2 proof inventory) is something pure-play window dealers (Pelican, Newman, BM, US Window & Door) cannot match. The "while we're here, we can also fix the dry rot we find" angle is a structural advantage.
5. **"CSLB #537570. Active since 1988. Verify at cslb.ca.gov."** Tier-1 document-as-proof. Forces the buyer to do the verification themselves — converts skepticism into discovery. Almost no chain ad shows its license number; even fewer invite verification.

These five open lanes are the creative wedge for Round 1. Every concept Agent 6 architects should pull from at least one of them and cannot pull from any of the five saturated lanes.
