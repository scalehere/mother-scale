# Facebook Ads for Home Services

Facebook (Meta) ads work for home service contractors, but via interruption rather than intent. Unlike Google, which captures people actively searching, Facebook reaches people who may need services but haven't started looking yet.

## When to Use

- Retargeting website visitors who didn't convert
- Seasonal promotions (time 4–6 weeks before peak season)
- Brand awareness within a defined service area
- Building audiences for future campaigns

## When NOT to Use

- Total ad budget under $1,500/month (put it all in Google/LSAs instead)
- No Meta Pixel installed on website
- No system to respond to leads within 5–15 minutes
- Running boosted posts instead of proper Ads Manager campaigns

## Best Ad Formats (Ranked)

1. **Before/after photos** — highest performing; visual transformation stops the scroll
2. **Retargeting ads** — warmest audience; 70% more likely to convert (per WordStream)
3. **Seasonal offer ads** — specific price + deadline drives action
4. **Lead form ads** — zero-friction submission; good for estimates/inspections
5. **Video testimonials** — 30-second real customer video builds trust fast

## What Doesn't Work

- Generic branding ads (logo + phone number)
- Boosted posts without targeting
- Broad audience targeting (25–65, 30-mile radius)

## Budget Guidelines

- Sweet spot: $500–$2,000/month
- Meta minimum for lead gen: $20/day
- Should complement Google Ads, not replace them

## Key Constraint

Facebook leads are warm, not hot. They require faster follow-up (5–15 min) and often 2–3 calls to convert. See [[concepts/lead-response-time]].
