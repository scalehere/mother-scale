# Google Ads vs. Facebook Ads for Home Services

The core strategic distinction in home services paid advertising. These channels are complementary, not competing — they serve different buyer stages.

## The Mental Model

**Google = intent-based.** The buyer is actively searching. High urgency, high close rate.
**Facebook = interruption-based.** The buyer is scrolling, not searching. Lower urgency, requires nurturing.

> "A homeowner with a burst pipe searches Google. A homeowner with a 15-year-old HVAC system scrolls Facebook."

## Side-by-Side Comparison

| Factor | Google Ads | Facebook/Meta Ads |
|---|---|---|
| Buyer intent | High (searching now) | Low–medium (scrolling) |
| Ad format | Text-based | Visual: images, video, carousels |
| Avg CPL | $30–$75 | $35–$65 |
| Close rate | 25–45% | 10–25% |
| Best for | Emergency calls, active buyers | Awareness, retargeting, seasonal |
| Speed to first lead | 24–72 hours | 3–7 days |
| Funnel position | Bottom (ready to buy) | Top and middle |

## Strategic Rule

**Google first. Facebook second.**

If total digital budget is under $1,500/month, put everything into Google Ads and [[Local Services Ads]]. Facebook is a top-of-funnel complement — it amplifies an already-working Google strategy. It does not fix a broken one.

## How They Work Together

1. Google captures immediate demand (emergency calls, active searchers)
2. Facebook retargets people who visited the Google-driven website but didn't convert
3. Facebook builds awareness with people who haven't searched yet but will
4. Seasonal Facebook campaigns prime the market before Google demand spikes
