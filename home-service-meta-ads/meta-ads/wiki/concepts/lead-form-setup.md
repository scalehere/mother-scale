# Lead Form Setup — Facebook Instant Forms

Everything about building high-converting Facebook Instant Forms for home service lead generation.

---

## Form Type: More Volume vs. Higher Intent

**Use: More Volume**

Higher Intent adds a review screen and "slide to submit" — confuses elderly users (a large portion of home service buyers), reduces conversion rate. The qualifying questions you add create quality filtering without needing the extra screen.

---

## Friction Dial: Quality vs. Volume

The more friction you add, the higher your CPL but better your lead quality. Calibrate based on:

| Business Type | Friction Level | Approach |
|---|---|---|
| Low-ticket, high-volume (pressure washing, window cleaning) | Low | 2–3 simple questions, no price mention |
| Mid-ticket (HVAC, plumbing, electrical) | Medium | 3–4 qualifying questions, service type selection |
| High-ticket (remodeling, ADU, solar, pools, lighting) | High | Add minimum price acknowledgment question |

**The minimum price question** (Hunsaker): "Are you aware that our minimum pricing for this service starts at $999?" Forces pre-qualification. Increases CPL but eliminates tire kickers. Critical for high-ticket verticals.

---

## Form Structure (Full Template)

### 1. Intro Screen
- **Headline**: Service name + value prop. "Arizona's #1 HVAC Service" or "Get Your Free AC Tune-Up Estimate"
- **Image**: Job photo or branded truck
- **Description**: 3–5 bullet points of what they get
- **Color scheme**: Match brand colors if option available

### 2. Qualifying Questions (Multiple Choice)

Minimum 2–4 questions depending on vertical. Examples:

**Window Cleaning (Dimsey)**:
1. Is your property single story or double story?
2. How many bedrooms?
3. What sides would you like cleaned (inside/out)?
4. How would you like to be contacted?

**High-Ticket (Hunsaker — Lighting/Remodel)**:
1. Are you looking to install on your home or business?
2. Are you aware that our minimum pricing starts at $999?

**Pest Control / Recurring Services**:
1. What service are you interested in? (multiple choice: mosquito, quarterly pest, rodent, etc.)
2. How urgent is your situation?

**HVAC**:
1. Do you need repair or replacement?
2. How old is your current system?
3. Is your home single or multi-story?

### 3. Contact Info

| Field | Include? | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| First name | Yes | |
| Last name | Yes | |
| Phone number | Yes | Primary contact for speed-to-lead |
| Email | Yes | For follow-up sequences and retargeting |
| City | Yes (not full address) | Full address increases CPL significantly |
| Full address | Only if necessary | Reserve for high-ticket installs that need site visit |

**Do not make any fields optional** — you want this data.

### 4. Thank You Screen

**This is often overlooked — converts aggressive buyers immediately.**

- Headline: "Thanks! You're all set."
- Copy: "Feel free to call now for an additional $[X] off — just mention this post."
- Add: Click-to-call button (phone number)
- Result: The most motivated leads call you before you even follow up

## Privacy Policy Requirement

Facebook requires a privacy policy URL. Options:
- Use your website's privacy policy page
- Google "Facebook lead form privacy policy generator" — many free tools
- Any compliant privacy policy will pass Facebook's review

---

## Flexible Form Delivery

Leave on "Optimized" — this just controls delivery speed of leads, not quality. No impact on results.

---

## What to Track After Launch

- **CPL**: Benchmark varies by vertical (see [[concepts/cost-per-lead]])
- **Lead-to-appointment rate**: Target 20%+
- **Lead quality score**: Are they answering the phone? Booking quotes?
