# Retargeting

Showing ads to people who previously visited your website but didn't convert. For home service contractors on Facebook, this is the highest-value use of the platform.

## How It Works

1. Install the Meta Pixel on your website (required prerequisite)
2. Pixel fires when a visitor lands on any page
3. That visitor is added to a custom audience in Meta Ads Manager
4. Your ads appear in their Facebook/Instagram feed within hours of their visit
5. The homeowner who browsed your services at lunch sees your ad again at dinner

## Why It Works

Visitors who see retargeting ads are 70% more likely to convert than those who don't (WordStream). These are warm leads — people who already showed enough interest to visit your site. Retargeting recaptures them at low cost.

## For Home Services Specifically

Someone may visit a plumber's site during a minor problem, not call, and forget about it. A retargeting ad later that week — when the issue hasn't resolved — can be the nudge that converts the visit into a call.

## Requirements

- Meta Pixel installed and verified on the website
- Sufficient website traffic to build a meaningful audience (low-traffic sites may struggle)
- Budget: retargeting audiences are usually small, so costs are low
