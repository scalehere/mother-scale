# Ad Creative Types — Home Services Facebook Ads

Full taxonomy of creative formats that work (and don't work) for home service Facebook ads, synthesized across all wiki sources.

---

## Tier 1: Consistently High-Performing

### Owner Intro Video
See [[concepts/owner-intro-video-ad]] for full details.
- Best creative type across all sources. Works for every home service vertical.
- Owner on camera, branded uniform, in front of truck or job site.
- 30–60 seconds. Call out location → intro → differentiators → offer → CTA.

### Customer Testimonial Interview Video
- Second most powerful. 99% of competitors won't do it (friction of asking).
- 20-minute interview with happy customer at their property.
- Questions: What compelled you? Would you recommend us? What was the result? Why us over others?
- Film in front of properties that look like your target customer's home.
- Match demographics: film with Indian clients for Indian-heavy markets, women for female buyers, etc.
- Can cut clips into owner intro video or run standalone.

### Before/After Still Image
- Highest performing image type for most verticals.
- Split image: dirty/damaged state vs. clean/finished state.
- Works especially well for: window cleaning, pressure washing, painting, landscaping, lighting, pool service, HVAC (duct photos).
- **Avoid for junk removal** — "before" is trash, which is off-putting.

---

## Tier 2: Solid Performers

### Seasonal Offer Image/Video
- Specific service + specific price + specific deadline.
- Time 4–6 weeks before peak season.
- "AC tune-up: $89 before summer. Book by [date]."

### Lead Form Image Ads (AI-Generated Still)
- AI image tools (Manis AI, Gemini, Nano Banana Pro) + real photo → Facebook-ready creative.
- Prompt: "Running promo for $X off [service]. Here's my logo and colors. Make 2–3 Facebook-friendly still images. Ask me 5 clarifying questions before generating."
- Feed 3–4 different source photos → multiple variations.
- Back in rotation with Meta's Andromeda update (~40% of views are muted = still image works without sound).

### Video Testimonial Clips
- 30-second customer sound bite cut from testimonial interview.
- "They showed up on time, fixed the problem, the price was fair."
- Real faces + real voice = faster trust than any graphic.

---

## Tier 3: Situational

### Click-to-Call Ads
- Best for emergency verticals: wasp nests, rodent infestations, burst pipes, HVAC in heatwave.
- CPL: $15–$100. Must answer within seconds.
- Not scalable unless you have dedicated phone staff.
- See [[sources/facebook-ads-strategy-blew-up-home-service]].

### AI Video Ads
- Not recommended until you've proven owner intro works.
- [[Steve Hunsaker]] testing them at scale ($600K+ spend experience) but explicitly says: don't skip the basics to do this.
- Could become more relevant as AI video improves.

### Website Traffic Campaigns
- Low intent. Not for immediate lead generation.
- Use for: new market entry, building pixel/retargeting audiences.
- See [[sources/facebook-ads-strategy-blew-up-home-service]].

## What Doesn't Work

- **Generic branding ads** — logo + phone number + "serving your community since [year]"
- **Boosted posts** — no targeting, no conversion tracking, no optimization
- **Broad audience targeting** — "25-65, 30 miles" without creative targeting
- **AI-generated image backgrounds** — looks fake, turns people off
- **Advantage+ creative enhancements** (text improvement, image animation, enhanced CTA) — you lose visibility into what's actually working

---

## Creative Mix Recommendation

Per campaign launch:
- **Minimum**: 5–10 creatives (Hunsaker). Some sources say 4+ is fine.
- **Suggested mix**: 3–5 owner intro video variations (different settings/backgrounds) + 2–4 still image variations
- Add testimonial video once you have one
- Let algorithm find winners, then make variations of what's working
