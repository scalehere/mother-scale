# Lead Response Time

How quickly a contractor responds to an inbound lead is one of the largest levers in lead conversion — especially for Facebook leads, which are warm but not hot.

## The Benchmark

**Target: 5–15 minutes.**

Companies that respond within 5 minutes close leads at 3–5x the rate of companies that wait an hour. (Cited in source but no primary data link provided — worth verifying.)

## Why It Matters More for Facebook

Google leads are high-intent — the homeowner searched, clicked, called or filled a form. They're already in buying mode and will often wait.

Facebook leads submitted a form while scrolling. They may have forgotten about it 30 minutes later or already called a competitor. The warm-to-cold decay is faster.

## Operational Requirements

- Instant lead notifications (email + SMS to whoever answers phones)
- Someone available to call back during business hours immediately
- CRM or system to log and track response times
- After-hours strategy: auto-response + next-day priority callback
