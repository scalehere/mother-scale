# Ad Targeting Settings

For home service Facebook ads, targeting is almost entirely handled by the creative. These are the settings to configure (and not configure).

---

## What to Set

### Location (The Only Required Targeting)
- Type your service city/area name
- **Shrink to city-only** or set tight radius (not the default +25 miles Facebook fills in)
- Two methods:
  - **City-only** (Hunsaker): Type city, select "current city only"
  - **Exclusion pins** (Dimsey): Set radius, then drop exclusion pins around the perimeter

### Uncheck "Reach More People Likely to Respond"
This setting expands delivery outside your target geography. Uncheck it every time. Hunsaker got leads from Tucson (2 hours away) when he left it on.

---

## What NOT to Set

| Setting | Do NOT touch | Reason |
|---|---|---|
| Interest targeting | Leave blank | Creative self-selects the right audience |
| Age targeting | Optional (25+ only) | FB age data is inaccurate. Minimal impact. |
| Language | Leave blank | Creative language does the filtering |
| Detailed targeting | Leave blank | Usually hurts more than helps |

---

## Placements

- **Leave Advantage Plus on** for most campaigns
- Even with Advantage Plus, 90% of budget goes to FB feed, IG reels, IG feed, stories
- Only turn off specific placements if you're getting consistently low-quality leads from a specific placement (diagnose via placement breakdown)

---

## The Exposure Effect Requirement

For the local celebrity / exposure effect to work (see [[concepts/owner-intro-video-ad]]):
- Must run in a **small, concentrated geography**
- Scaled spend in a large market dilutes the effect
- Start with your richest neighborhoods, then expand
