# Owner Intro Video Ad

The single most consistently recommended ad format across all home service Facebook ads sources in this wiki. Called the "foolproof tried-and-true system that works across all home service verticals" by [[Steve Hunsaker]].

## The Universal Script Structure

```
1. CALL OUT THE LOCATION (first words out of your mouth)
   "Hey [City/Neighborhood] homeowners..."
   "Do you own a home in [City]?"
   OR visual hook showing the neighborhood/landmarks

2. INTRODUCE YOURSELF
   "My name is [Name]. I'm the owner of [Business]."

3. WHAT YOU DO + WHY YOU'RE DIFFERENT
   Brief. What service. Key differentiators (reviews, awards, years in business,
   number of trucks, specific technology).

4. EXPIRING OFFER
   A specific discount, promo, or free service with a reason it's available now.
   "We have a [month] special — $X off."
   "A few spots opened up this week."

5. CALL TO ACTION
   "Click the 'Get Quote' button below to redeem this offer."
```

**Length**: 30–60 seconds. 60 seconds absolute maximum. Shorter = cheaper placement, higher completion rate.

## Non-Negotiables for Trust Building

- **Branded uniform** — logo visible on chest. No white t-shirt, no casual clothes.
- **Branded vehicle in background** — wrapped truck or van. Subconscious legitimacy signal.
- **On-location filming** — in front of a customer's house (ideally nice), at a job site, in front of your shop, or all three.
- **Subtitles** — ~40% of viewers watch muted. Add via CapCut, not Meta AI (retain control).
- **Selfie style or tripod** — no professional crew needed. iPhone + $15 Amazon lapel mic + $30 tripod.

## Why It Works (Psychology)

**Local anchoring**: First words = neighborhood name. Brain registers "this person is my neighbor, not a random advertiser."

**Exposure effect**: Facebook shows the ad repeatedly until conversion. Frequency score builds. Over time, the owner becomes a "local celebrity" — people recognize you at the coffee shop, bank, etc. Requires small targeted area + scaled spend to achieve this effect.

**Humanization**: Face on camera + real voice + real location removes the "paid advertisement" filter people apply to most ads.

**Trust building**: Uniform + truck + reviews + awards = subconscious "this person has done this a thousand times."

## Variations as You Get More Advanced

- Open with a visual hook (show the result — lit-up house at night) rather than auditory callout
- Cut to a customer testimonial mid-ad before the offer
- Film the same script in different settings (in front of truck, at customer's house, at job site) — test which setting wins, not just the script

## What to Wear

Professional branded uniform. The casualness of how you speak = humanization. The professionalism of how you look = trust. Both matter. "Are you buttoned up to the point that a stay-at-home mom feels comfortable with you showing up to her house?"

## Equipment

- iPhone (better than 30-year-old Hollywood cameras)
- Lapel/lavalier microphone ($50–$300) — audio quality matters more than video quality
- Tripod ($15–$30 Amazon) — optional for selfie style, recommended for straight-on shots
- CapCut for editing + subtitles
