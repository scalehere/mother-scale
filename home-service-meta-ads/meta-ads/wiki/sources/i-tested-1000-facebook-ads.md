# I Tested 1,000+ Facebook Ads, These 3 Print For Home Service Businesses

## Abstract

Steve Hunsaker ($600K+ personal ad spend, 700+ client businesses) breaks down the three proven creative types with full psychological reasoning. This is the most comprehensive creative framework in the wiki. Key addition vs. earlier videos: still image ads are back (Andromeda update), testimonial interviews are deeply underused, and AI image tools make still image production fast.

## The Three Creative Types

### 1. Owner Intro Video
See [[concepts/owner-intro-video-ad]] for full details.

**Why it works:**
- **Exposure effect** — repeated local exposure builds familiarity. Like Coca-Cola in a local market. If you run in a small, rich area and scale spend, you eventually become "the local brand."
- **Humanizing** — selfie style, face on camera, in-market setting removes the "advertiser on a screen" feeling.
- **Local anchoring** — first words = neighborhood name. Brain says "this person is my neighbor."

**Requirement for exposure effect**: Must run in small, targeted area (rich neighborhoods preferred) with scaled spend. $50/day across a 7M person market = no exposure effect.

### 2. Customer Testimonial Interview Video
**Why it works:**
- **Social proof** — the most powerful psychological trigger. Referral leads close at 2–3x because of social proof.
- **Narrative transportation** — viewers catapult themselves into the testimonial-giver's position when the criteria (demographics, lifestyle, house type) match.
- **Not affiliated bias** — a customer saying "they were amazing" is 100x more credible than the owner saying it.

**How to get testimonials:**
- Champion customers (realtors, networkers, anyone who wants to collaborate)
- Established businesses: offer a bottle of wine, come with a camera and microphone
- Ask: What compelled you? Would you recommend? What was the result? Why us over competitors?

**Critical nuance — demographic matching:**
- Film testimonials that look like your target customer
- If targeting affluent homeowners, film in front of nice houses (not your smallest jobs)
- If your market is diverse, get diverse testimonials — Indian clients, women, different ages
- People resonate with people who look like them

**Barrier to entry**: 99% of competitors will never do this — friction of asking + filming stops them. Huge competitive advantage.

### 3. Still Image Ads (Back with Andromeda Update)
**Why it's back:**
- ~40% of Facebook ads consumed with phone on mute. Still image works without sound.
- Meta's Andromeda algorithm update improved still image distribution.

**How to make them (AI-assisted):**
1. Take a real photo (technician on-site, before/after, crew in front of truck)
2. Go to Manis AI, Gemini, or Nano Banana Pro
3. Prompt: "We're running a promo for $X off [service]. Here is my logo and colors. Make 2–3 Facebook-friendly still image ads. Ask me 5 clarifying questions before generating."
4. Feed 3–4 different source photos → get multiple variations

**Best still image types:**
- Technician with product/equipment (logo visible)
- Before/after side-by-side (avoid for junk removal — "before" is trash)
- Crew in front of branded truck
- Finished product (exterior lighting, remodel, etc.)

**Testing**: Launch 10 still images at once, let algorithm find the winner, then make variations of the winner.

## How Many Creatives to Launch

**Minimum: 5–10 creatives per campaign.** Ideally some videos + some still images.
- Videos need subtitles (CapCut preferred over Meta's AI subtitles — more control)
- "If you launch with one creative and fail, that's a skill issue, not a platform issue."

## Advanced: When to Go Off-Script

Don't test AI ads or unusual formats until you've proven the owner intro works. Once you have a winning campaign, test variations. Order matters:
1. Prove owner intro → scale
2. Add testimonials
3. Test still images
4. Only then experiment with AI video, unusual formats
