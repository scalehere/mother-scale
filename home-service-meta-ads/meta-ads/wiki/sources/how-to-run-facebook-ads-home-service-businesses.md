# How to Run Facebook Ads for Your Home Service Businesses

## Abstract

Comprehensive step-by-step campaign setup tutorial from Steve Hunsaker (Home Service Accelerator), who has helped 550+ home service businesses run their own ads. Covers the full flow from creative structure → Ads Manager setup → lead form build. The core format is the owner intro video with expiring offer. Key emphasis: systems (speed-to-lead, follow-up) matter as much as ads.

## Key Claims

- **Owner intro video with expiring offer** is the universal template that works across all home service verticals.
- Branded uniform + branded vehicle in video = subconscious trust building. Non-negotiable.
- **Campaign budget at campaign level** (not ad set) — lets Facebook allocate to the best-performing ad automatically.
- **Uncheck "Reach more people likely to respond"** — prevents Facebook from going outside your service area.
- **Narrow city targeting** — type city name, shrink radius to city-only or tight radius. Don't let it default to +25 miles.
- **Turn off all enhancements** in the ad creative settings — text improvement, image animation, enhanced CTA, etc. You lose visibility into what's actually working.
- **Turn off multi-advertiser ads** — don't let your ad appear in clusters with competitors.
- **No detailed targeting needed** — creative finds the audience. Language/interest targeting usually hurts more than helps.
- **Form friction for quality** — adding minimum charge acknowledgment question reduced lead volume but dramatically improved lead quality.
- **"Get Quote" CTA** outperforms alternatives (Book Now, Learn More, Get in Touch).
- **After form submit**: prompt to call for additional discount — converts the most aggressive buyers immediately.
- Systems are essential: speed to lead + same-day/next-day quote turnaround + follow-up sequences = results. Ads alone aren't enough.
- $500K in sales in one month, $1M+ in a year from this strategy.

## Campaign Setup (Step-by-Step)

1. **Campaign objective**: Leads
2. **Campaign budget**: Set at campaign level (e.g., $100/day), not ad set level
3. **Ad set**: Name it, leave start date as-is, ignore catalog
4. **Location**: City name only. Shrink radius to city boundaries. Uncheck "reach more people" button.
5. **Age**: Optional, 25+ only if relevant. Note: Facebook age data is often inaccurate.
6. **Detailed targeting**: None. Creative finds the audience.
7. **Placements**: Leave Advantage Plus on (Facebook optimizes across feed, reels, stories)
8. **Ad**: Turn off multi-advertiser ads
9. **Creative**: Upload video. Set primary text, headline, description, CTA = "Get Quote"
10. **Enhancements**: Turn ALL off
11. **Lead form**: More Volume. Add qualifying questions. Contact info: first name, last name, phone, city (not full address). Privacy policy required.
12. **Form thank-you screen**: "Thanks, you're all set. Call now for $X additional off — mention this post." Include click-to-call button.

## Lead Form Structure

- Intro: headline + image + description (bullet points of what they get)
- Question 1: Multiple choice qualifying question (home vs. business, or service interest)
- Question 2 (optional friction): Minimum price acknowledgment — e.g., "Are you aware our minimum pricing starts at $999?"
- Contact info: First name, last name, phone, city (not full address — increases CPL too much)
- Thank you screen with click-to-call
