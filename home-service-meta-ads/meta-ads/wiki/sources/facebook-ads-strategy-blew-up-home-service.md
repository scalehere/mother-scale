# This Facebook Ads Strategy BLEW UP My Home Service Business (Copy Me)

## Abstract

Jonas Olson (pest control operator, scaled from 1 to 17 locations across 5 states) shares a campaign objectives framework and a 7-step ad copy formula that he applies across Facebook, Google, YouTube, direct mail, and door hangers. Most unique contribution to the wiki: the click-to-call campaign type for emergency/high-intent situations, and the PS/PSS structure for ad copy.

## Three Campaign Objectives and When to Use Each

### 1. Website Traffic
- **Intent**: Very low
- **CPL**: $0.20–$0.30 (cost per click, not lead)
- **Goal**: Brand awareness, retargeting seed
- **Best for**: New market entry, warming up cold audiences, building pixel data
- **Warning**: Don't expect immediate ROI. Website must have clear CTA and convert.
- **Feeds into**: Retargeting campaigns (past 30/60/90 days)

### 2. Click-to-Call
- **Intent**: Very high — nobody calls pest control for fun
- **CPL**: $15–$100 (stop if over $100)
- **Best for**: Emergency situations — rodent infestations, wasp nests, termite concerns, army worms, grub damage, any emergency pest situation
- **Warning**: Must answer within seconds. Have a sales script ready. Not for busy seasons when you can't staff phones.

### 3. Lead Forms (Preferred)
- **Intent**: Medium-high
- **CPL**: $12–$40
- **Best for**: Recurring services (quarterly pest control, mosquito, fertilization, window cleaning plans), anything reoccurring
- **Speed to lead**: Call within 5 minutes or lead is dead. They're on their phone when they fill out the form — call them then.
- **Scalable and predictable** — best for growing an agency or multi-location business

## The 7-Step Ad Copy Framework

Applies universally: Facebook, Google, YouTube, postcards, door hangers.

```
1. CREATIVE HOOK — stop the scroll within 2–3 seconds. Movement, transformation, before/after.

2. LOCAL CALLOUT — "Hey OKC homeowners" / "Hey Glenwick County homeowners" / "Hey [HOA name] homeowners"
   → They think: "This ad is for me."

3. PAIN POINT — name the specific problem they're already experiencing.
   Match to what your actual customers in that area are dealing with.
   Examples: ants in the kitchen, mice in the garage, weeds taking over, wasps by the back door.

4. YOUR SOLUTION — explain how you solve it. Brief. Don't need full process.
   Just enough to establish confidence.

5. WHY IT MATTERS (emotional) — safety for kids/pets, protecting their home's value,
   peace of mind. More emotional than technical.

6. SOCIAL PROOF (5-star review) — bonus: use a review from someone in that same
   neighborhood dealing with that same pest/problem.

7. CLEAR CTA — one action only. "Call now." "Click the link below." No multiple options.

THEN: PS / PSS — offer, urgency, or guarantee.
   - "PS: Book this week and get first treatment 50% off."
   - "PS: We only service your area until Friday."
   - "PS: If weeds come back, we come back free."
   People read the headline → drop to the PS. This is where the offer lives.
```

## Frequency and Touchpoints

- People need to see you **21–22 times** before they buy (cold traffic)
- More touchpoints across channels = faster conversion
- Retarget website visitors on Facebook after driving traffic via other channels
- "The more they see you, the better chance they'll buy"
