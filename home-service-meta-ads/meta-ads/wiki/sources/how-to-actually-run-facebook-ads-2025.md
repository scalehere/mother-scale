# How To Actually Run Facebook Ads 2025 (Home Service Businesses)

## Abstract

Window cleaner turned ads educator walks through his exact campaign setup from scratch. Advocates a stripped-down structure: 1 campaign, 1 ad set, 2 ads (only the image differs). Personal face-forward image ads outperform everything. Instant forms with 4 qualifying questions pre-filter lead quality without needing "higher intent" form type.

## Key Claims

- **Personal face ads are the #1 format** — owner wearing work uniform, introducing themselves. "These personal ads work unbelievably well for home service businesses."
- **Image > video** for simplicity and speed. Don't overcomplicate creative.
- **Ad copy matters less than the image** — he's run nearly the same copy for 2 years. Image does 90% of the heavy lifting.
- **Exclude surrounding area pins** to prevent Facebook showing ads outside your service area. Takes 10 seconds, saves significant budget.
- **Form type: More Volume** (not Higher Intent) — the form's qualifying questions create quality filtering anyway. Higher Intent's "slide to submit" confuses elderly users.
- **No detailed interest targeting** — location only. Facebook's algorithm knows who converts; don't constrain it with age/interest filters.
- **Split test structure**: 1 campaign → 1 ad set → 2 ads, only the image changes. Cleanest test.
- **Budget**: ~$35–$75 USD/day (~$50–$100 AUD) at campaign level.

## Campaign Setup Steps

1. Campaign objective: **Leads**
2. Performance goal: **Maximize number of leads**
3. Budget: ~$35–75/day at campaign level
4. Conversion: **Instant forms**
5. Audience: Location only (drop pin + radius). Exclude surrounding pins manually.
6. No age targeting, no interest targeting
7. Ad creative: Single image, personal/face-forward
8. Form type: **More Volume** with 4 qualifying questions

## Lead Form Structure (Window Cleaning Example)

4 multiple-choice qualifying questions before contact details:
1. Is your property single story or double story?
2. How many bedrooms?
3. What sides would you like cleaned (inside/out)?
4. How would you like to be contacted?

Then: first name, email, phone, street address.

## Ad Copy Structure

- Intro: "Hey everyone, I'm [Name]. I've been [service] in [location] for [X] years."
- Light urgency: "A few people have rescheduled, so I've got spots open."
- Vague discount: "Massive discount" (no price — lets imagination work). Nobody ever asks.
- Guarantee: 100% satisfaction or no charge.
- Headline: "[Get your X] done" — direct action statement.
- Description: Social proof — "X+ 5-star Google reviews" or "X+ happy customers."
- CTA: "Get Quote"
