# Do Facebook Ads Work for Home Service Companies?

## Abstract

Facebook ads work for home service companies, but they operate differently than Google Ads. Google is intent-based (catching people searching now); Facebook is interruption-based (reaching people who may need services but haven't searched yet). The article argues contractors should run Google Ads first to capture bottom-of-funnel demand, then layer Facebook on top for retargeting, seasonal promotions, and brand awareness.

## Key Claims

- **Google first, Facebook second**: If total budget is under $1,500/month, put everything into Google Ads and Local Services Ads. Facebook is a top-of-funnel complement, not a replacement.
- **Best Facebook ad formats** (ranked): before/after photos > retargeting ads > seasonal offer ads > lead form ads > video testimonials.
- **Worst practices**: generic branding ads, boosted posts, broad audience targeting.
- **Budget sweet spot**: $500–$2,000/month on Facebook, alongside (not instead of) Google spend.
- **Lead response time is critical**: Respond within 5–15 minutes. Companies responding in 5 min close at 3–5x the rate of those waiting an hour.
- **Meta Pixel**: Must be installed on website before running any Facebook campaigns to enable retargeting.
- **Minimum daily budget**: Meta recommends $20/day for lead generation campaigns to give the algorithm enough data.

## CPL Benchmarks

| Vertical | Facebook CPL | Google Ads CPL |
|---|---|---|
| HVAC | $40–$70 | $35–$60 |
| Plumbing | $35–$60 | $30–$55 |
| Electrical | $30–$55 | $25–$50 |
| Landscaping | $25–$45 | $20–$40 |
| Tree Service | $30–$55 | $35–$85 |

Note: Facebook CPLs are competitive, but close rates are lower (10–25%) vs. Google (25–45%) because Facebook leads are earlier in the buying process and may require 2–3 follow-up calls.

## Platform Comparison

| Factor | Google Ads | Facebook/Meta Ads |
|---|---|---|
| Buyer intent | High (searching now) | Low–medium (scrolling) |
| Ad format | Text | Visual: images, video, carousels |
| Avg CPL | $30–$75 | $35–$65 |
| Close rate | 25–45% | 10–25% |
| Best for | Emergency calls, active buyers | Awareness, retargeting, seasonal |
| Speed to first lead | 24–72 hours | 3–7 days |
| Funnel position | Bottom | Top and middle |

## Notable Quotes

> "Google catches people searching right now. Facebook builds awareness and generates leads from people who need your services but haven't searched yet."

> "A homeowner with a burst pipe searches Google. A homeowner with a 15-year-old HVAC system scrolls Facebook."

## The Recommended Playbook

1. Run Google Ads and LSAs first
2. Install Meta Pixel on website
3. Start with retargeting campaigns
4. Add seasonal campaigns (4–6 weeks before peak season)
5. Use before/after photos and video testimonials
6. Respond to leads within minutes
