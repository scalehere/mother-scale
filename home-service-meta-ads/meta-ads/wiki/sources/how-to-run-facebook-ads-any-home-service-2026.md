# How to Run Facebook Ads For ANY Home Service Business in 2026

## Abstract

Spencer Claeys runs a home service business (permanent/holiday lights, roof cleaning, gutter cleaning) and shares his video-first Facebook ad approach. Key insight: Facebook is now a "shotgun" platform — broad creative testing beats precise targeting. The ad script framework centers on calling out the customer, identifying the pain, and making a simple offer with a reason.

## Key Claims

- **Video is preferred over images** — "captures the eye" better, though images can work.
- **The shotgun approach has replaced sniper targeting** — can't do intense demographic targeting anymore. It's about the creative finding the right person.
- **$10/day minimum** to get started. Suggests splitting $20–$30 across multiple video variations.
- **Multiple videos > few videos** — he advocates 5–7+ video variations so the algorithm can find the best performer. Most people quit with 2 videos and call it not working.
- **Messaging/engagement campaign** — his preferred objective (vs. leads), used for getting messages/conversations. Gets ~$7–10 per message in off-season.
- **FB Ads Library** is free competitive intelligence — shows what angles competitors are using, what's working.
- **Winning ads run indefinitely** — if it's working in August, keep running the February ad. Don't stop winners.
- **ChatGPT for script ideation** — use for idea generation and angle testing, never take output verbatim.

## Ad Script Framework

```
1. Hook: Call out the customer OR name the problem
   - "Homeowners who love holidays but hate hanging lights..."
   - "Are your gutters clogged?" / "Is your roof dirty?"

2. Identity/intro: Who you are, what you do

3. Explain why / what they should be getting done

4. Simple offer with a reason
   - "We have a January special — 10% off"
   - Reason: "We have leftover product from holiday season"

5. Repeat the offer / CTA
```

## Ads Library Competitive Research

- Go to facebook.com/ads/library
- Search your service type (e.g., "permanent lights", "gutter cleaning")
- Study the angles being used
- Find angles that hit pain points (e.g., "Homeowners who love holidays but hate hanging lights")
- Take to ChatGPT: "Follow this script loosely as you make my ad"

## Open Questions

- Uses engagement/messaging objective rather than leads — different from most other sources who use lead forms. Worth testing both to compare quality.
- "$10/day" budget is very low — likely works for small markets or off-season testing only.
