# Spencer Claeys

Home service business owner (permanent/holiday lights, roof cleaning, gutter cleaning) and content creator. Video-first philosophy. Emphasizes the shotgun approach: test many video variations, let the algorithm find the winner. Uses engagement/messaging campaigns rather than lead forms.

## Key Perspective

Creative volume > precise targeting. Facebook has become a shotgun platform — the creative finds the person, not your interest targeting. FB Ads Library is underused for competitive research.
