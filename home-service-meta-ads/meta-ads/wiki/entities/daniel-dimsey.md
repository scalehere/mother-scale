# Daniel Dimsey

Home service business owner (window cleaning, Australia) and Facebook ads educator. Runs his own window cleaning business and ads for other home service businesses. Teaches a simplified approach: personal face ads, image > video, minimal targeting, instant forms with qualifying questions.

## Key Perspective

Stripped-down approach. 1 campaign, 1 adset, 2 image ads. Emphasizes that ad copy matters far less than the image. Personal face + work uniform = best performing creative type.
