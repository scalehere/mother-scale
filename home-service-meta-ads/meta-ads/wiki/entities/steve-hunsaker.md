# Steve Hunsaker

Founder of [[Home Service Accelerator]]. Owner of Valley Christmas Lights (exterior/permanent lighting, Scottsdale AZ). Has spent $600K+ on Facebook ads personally and helped 800+ home service businesses implement their own ad strategies. The most prolific home services Facebook ads educator in the wiki.

## Key Perspective

Owner intro video is the foundational creative type. Branding (uniform, truck) is non-negotiable. CPL is a vanity metric — track cost per appointment and CAC. Minimum 4–8 creatives. Systems (speed-to-lead, follow-up) are equally important as ads.

## Businesses

- Valley Christmas Lights — did $1.17M+ in one season via Facebook/Instagram ads
- Home Service Accelerator — education/consulting program for home service business owners
