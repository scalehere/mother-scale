## What this wiki covers

A 11-source corpus on running Meta (Facebook + Instagram) ads for home-service / contracting businesses. 10 of 11 sources are by [[Steve Hunsaker]] (founder of [[Home Service Accelerator]], operator of [[Valley Christmas Lights]] and [[Valley Premier Lighting]]). One source is a 2025 blog post by [[Mandelyn Miller]] of [[Hook Agency]] — the only contrasting voice in the corpus and a useful counter-reference for terminology a contractor might encounter elsewhere.

## The unifying thesis

The corpus argues that home-service operators should stop paying $1K–$2K/month agencies for mediocre Meta campaigns and instead build the skill in-house. Because once Meta's algorithm pivoted post-iOS to creative-driven targeting (see [[Creative Beats Targeting]]), the work agencies used to do — interest selection, audience tuning — is largely automated. What's left is creative production, and that work falls on the owner anyway because no agency can fake an authentic owner-facing video.

## The strategic frame: [[Tri-Pillar Marketing System]]

The flagship framework. Three customer-acquisition pillars must run in parallel:

1. **Paid** — Meta ads (always in-house) + Google PPC (always via agency, $2K–$4K/month).
2. **Organic** — [[Google Maps SEO]] driven by [[Photo Reviews]], synced socials, and frequent owner posts.
3. **Physical** — yard signs, A-frames, gift cards, vehicle wraps, postcard mailers.

The [[Bonfire Analogy]] explains the dynamic: brand is the fire pit, organic is the slow-burning log, paid is the gasoline. Pour gas alone and you ride the [[Agency Hamster Wheel]] — burn out the moment spend stops. Pour gas onto a slow-burning organic log inside a real brand fire pit and the channels compound.

[[Valley Christmas Lights]] documents the long-run case: paid share dropped 95% → ~45% of new business across 5 years as organic compounded. Margin grew because free calls cost nothing.

## The Meta playbook

**Creative is the lever, not targeting.** Three creative archetypes:

1. [[Owner-Facing Selfie Video Ad]] — 30–60s, branded uniform + branded vehicle, [[Six-Step Ad Structure]] (city callout → name → what → why-different → expiring offer → audible CTA).
2. [[Testimonial Ad]] — customer-interview, demographically-matched ([[Narrative Transportation]]), 99% of competitors won't make one because it's uncomfortable to ask.
3. [[Still Image Ads]] — back in 2026 because ~40% of Facebook ads are watched on mute. AI-generated via the [[AI-Generated Creative Workflow]] with the signature *"please ask me five clarifying questions before generating"* prompt.

**Operational rules that recur in every source:**

- Never publish a campaign with one creative ([[Creative Variation]]). Floor: 4. Ideal: 4–8. Sometimes 10–20 for advanced operators.
- [[Lead Form Friction]] (e.g. minimum-price acknowledgement) raises CPL but stabilizes lead quality. CPL is a **vanity metric**; track [[Marketing Math (CPL → CPA → CAC)]] instead.
- [[Speed to Lead Automation]] is non-negotiable. 5-minute auto-call + voicemail drop + SMS sequence → 6–10× ROAS. 48-hour callbacks → campaign looks broken.
- [[Sniper-Rifle Geographic Targeting]] beats shotgun. Concentration drives [[Exposure Effect]], referral density, and [[Brand-Build + Lead-Gen Dual Purpose]] mindshare.
- Scale budget at [[Scaling Cadence (15-25% per week)]] — bigger jumps reset the algorithm's learning phase.

## Channel comparison

[[Google Ads vs Facebook Ads]] establishes the canonical decision frame: Meta = cheap CPL, lower-quality leads, [[Interruption-Style Marketing]], doubles as brand builder, owner runs in-house. Google PPC = expensive, high-intent, requires daily management, agency-only ($2–4K/month retainer + % of spend ideal). Below ~$50K/month revenue, start Meta-only. Above that, layer Google PPC.

## Open threads / contradictions

- **Hook Agency vs. Hunsaker on agencies.** [[Hook Agency]] is more agency-positive (logical — they sell agency services for SEO/website/Google Ads). Both agree on the underlying tactics; they disagree on whether DIY is realistic for the median owner.
- **Hook Agency on lookalikes** is more emphatic than Hunsaker, who barely mentions [[Lookalike Audiences]] — likely because his thesis is that broad geo + creative does the same job better post-Andromeda.
- **Boosted posts.** Hook Agency permits them for visibility; Hunsaker dismisses them in favor of always running campaigns through Ads Manager.
