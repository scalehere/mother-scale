[[Steve Hunsaker]] (published 2025-12-26). The most operational source in the corpus — a click-by-click setup walkthrough that the [[Complete Marketing Guide]] partly excerpts. Includes the [[Ledesma Remodeling]] $11K → $400K ad reference and Steve's own Christmas-lights ad with a $999 minimum-acknowledgement friction question.

## Key Claims

### Creative
- Owner-intro template with expiring value prop is the foolproof structure across trades. Branded uniform + branded vehicle in the shot is non-negotiable. Audio quality matters more than video quality — buy a $50–300 phone-plug lapel mic.
- Keep videos 30s–~1m. Visual hooks (text on screen showing the city) can substitute for the audible neighborhood callout.

### Campaign Setup
- Conversion event: **leads** (not messaging, not phone calls). Campaign-budget level (~$100/day starter) so the algorithm reallocates across creatives automatically.
- Locations: dial radius down or use **current city only** — uncheck "reach more people likely to respond" or you'll pay for leads 2 hours away.
- Skip detailed interest targeting. Leave [[Advantage+ Placements]] on. Don't touch language settings. Set age 25+ if relevant but don't over-narrow.
- Ignore campaign score. Disable multi-advertiser ads. Turn all "enhancements" off.

### Lead Form
- Form ad with custom intro (image, headline, bullet description). Use multiple-choice friction questions — Steve's are "residential or commercial?" and "are you aware our minimum is $999?". Without friction his CPL would drop ~80% but quality would collapse.
- Collect first name, last name, email, phone, **city** (not full address — full address tanks volume disproportionately).
- Privacy policy: just Google one and link.
- Thank-you page must say "Feel free to call now for an additional $100 off — just mention this post" with a tap-to-call button to capture the high-intent buyers.

### Systems Behind It
- Speed-to-lead automation is mandatory. Auto-call attempt → SMS → voicemail drop → 5 follow-up texts → email nurture. Without it, "Facebook ads don't work" is really "your systems don't work."
- 550+ HSA businesses; the failure mode of every "Facebook ads don't work" complaint is a single-creative campaign at $25/day with no follow-up. "Facebook is a damn near trillion dollar company for a reason."
