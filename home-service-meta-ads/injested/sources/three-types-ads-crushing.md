[[Steve Hunsaker]] (published 2025-10-30). Earliest video in the corpus. Sets up the theme that recurs across every later source: video ads built around the [[Six-Step Ad Structure]] generate leads *and* build local brand simultaneously, and that combo is the moat against agency-using competitors.

## Key Claims

- **Why video.** Subtitles required (half the audience watches on mute). Six-step structure = neighborhood → who you are → what you do → why different → expiring offer → audible CTA.
- **Front-end profitable** is the bar: $1,500/month spend → ~$5K back = ~3.3× ROAS, "decent." Once profitable, scale the spend — the people who *don't* convert still see the brand 3–5×, follow on Instagram, or Google you later. Story: $30K customer attributed to "organic Google" in the CRM, but on-site told the rep she'd been seeing the funny Instagram ads. See [[Brand-Build + Lead-Gen Dual Purpose]].
- **Agencies under $2K/month are typically bad.** Cheap agency math: $1K/month retainer + $25/day spend = 57% of marketing dollars never reach Meta. Owner films the creative anyway, so the agency really just clips and uploads. Better: take that whole $1,750 to Meta yourself and develop the skill in-house. "Good agencies are $2–4K/month and target 6-figure-monthly businesses."
- **Skill ladder.** Grandpa boosting a post → AI slop still images → owner video unscripted → owner video scripted with structure → owner video + Fiverr editor with B-roll → expert (HSA / YouTube). Each rung is real local advantage. See [[Skill Gap]].
- **The 3–5 creative rule** is justified mechanically: Meta's algorithm reallocates daily budget toward winners within ~24–48 hours; remaining creatives serve as nurture content for prospects who need 2–3 touches before converting.
- **Brand vs. lead-gen.** Lead-gen is instant gratification, easy to talk about. Brand is hard, slow, and ignored — but is what determines whether the stay-at-home mom feels safe with you on her doorstep. Branded polos for sales reps, $200 iPad for on-site quotes, branded thank-you flow, review nurturers, referral incentives — every detail is a tax against your competitors.
