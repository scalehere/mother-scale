The only non-Hunsaker source in the corpus — a 2025 blog post from [[Hook Agency]] by [[Mandelyn Miller]]. More structured/agency-flavored than the YouTube material; useful as a counterpoint and for terminology a contractor might encounter elsewhere.

## Key Claims

- **What changed in 2026:** iOS privacy changes pushed reliance onto first-party data (CRM, email lists, [[Meta Pixel]] + [[Conversion API]]). Short-form video and Reels dominate over static images. AI tools — [[Advantage+ Placements]], automated A/B testing, budget recommendations — quietly do more of the targeting work.
- **Winning ad types:** lead ads with short forms ("Book Now" / "Get a Quote Today"); before/after job videos with voiceover; offers with real urgency and short deadlines; testimonial ads (video clips or graphic posts with headshot + quote, repurposed Google reviews).
- **Budget tips:** geo-target by ZIP/radius, exclude unserved areas, schedule ads during business hours, run separate ad sets per service zone, A/B test inside Ads Manager Experiments, build [[Lookalike Audiences]] from a custom-audience seed of 100–5,000 past customers/CRM leads. Boosts are fine for visibility; real campaigns are where ROI lives.
- **Common mistakes:** targeting too wide or too narrow, no follow-up strategy (connect lead ads → CRM/email, assign someone, auto-replies), generic stock-photo creative, skipping Pixel + Conversion API setup → flying blind.
- **DIY vs. agency:** DIY works if hands-on and willing to learn; most contractors hit a ceiling juggling sales + ops + marketing and lose more in dropped leads than agency fees. Hook Agency itself doesn't run Facebook ads — they pitch website / SEO / Google Ads instead.
