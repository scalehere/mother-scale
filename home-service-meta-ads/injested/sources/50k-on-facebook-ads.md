[[Steve Hunsaker]] (published 2026-01-05). Personal retrospective: $50K spent across [[Valley Christmas Lights]] + [[Valley Premier Lighting]] over ~14 months returned ~$400K (≈8× direct ROAS). Frames the journey from $200–300K → $1.1M annual sales.

## Key Claims

### Lesson 1: Creative beats targeting
- iOS opt-out broke Meta's tracking → Meta rebuilt the algorithm so **creative does the targeting**. Setting ages, incomes, interests is mostly cargo-cult. What matters is what's said in the ad to make the right people self-select.
- What works: owner on camera, in uniform, in front of the branded vehicle, talking about who/what/why-different + value prop. What doesn't: AI slop images, $40 Fiverr cartoons, no face, no brand.

### Lesson 2: Don't get emotionally attached to ads
- Showed a thoughtful "Arizona summers are hot, here's how we keep our crews safe" ad that flopped — and a less-flashy "feeling held hostage by outdated holiday lighting" ad that crushed. The algorithm decides; the operator's job is to feed it 4–7 variations.

### Lesson 3: Speed and follow-up determine whether the campaign works
- Same 100 leads → 48-hour response = dead. 5-minute auto-call + voicemail drop + automated SMS sequence = converts. Walks through a real CRM trail: opt-in → attempted call → voicemail + SMS → 1-hour follow-up SMS → next-day "Keep us posted" → reply "I would like a quote" → automation cancels next steps → quote → close $5K.
- Difference between 2–3× ROAS and 6–10× ROAS is purely systems and follow-up after the creative is shipped.

### Lesson 4: Scale slowly, not aggressively
- 15–25% per week is the cap. Jumping from $50/day → $500/day kills performance because the audience and pixel can't adjust.

### Lesson 5: Blended ROAS > direct ROAS
- At peak ($400–500/day), CPL creeps but **brand mindshare** explodes locally. Story: barber, IV bar staff, banker all see his ads. Real example — closed a job that the CRM attributed to a postcard, but the customer also had the yard sign across the street and had seen the Facebook ads. Direct ROAS understates the channel; track [[Blended ROAS / Customer Acquisition Cost]] across all marketing once spend grows.
- Macro: ~190M Americans on Facebook weekly, ~169M on Instagram. "Facebook ads don't work" = skill issue, not platform issue.
