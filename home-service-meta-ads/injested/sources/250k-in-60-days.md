YouTube video by [[Steve Hunsaker]] (published 2026-03-30) breaking down how remodeling companies in [[Home Service Accelerator]] are landing six-figure jobs from Facebook Ads. Centerpieces: [[Ledesma Remodeling]] (sold a six-figure remodel after $600 in spend, scaled to ~30x ROAS after testing 60–70 creatives) and [[Ash Harbor Remodeling]] (six-figure job off their *first* Facebook ad).

## Key Claims

- High-ticket trades (remodelers, roofers, plumbers, pool builders, permanent lighting) win on Facebook because each "at-bat" can produce a six-figure result; they fail when owners kill campaigns before the sales cycle (3 weeks–3 months) finishes.
- Authentic, owner-filmed video destroys Hollywood-polished ads. Visual map pop-ups showing the local city (e.g. "Today we're here in Gilbert") signal *this is your neighbor*.
- Three pillars classify any high-converting home-service ad: **educate, entertain, social proof** — see [[Educate-Entertain-Social-Proof Framework]]. The Ledesma ads stack educate + social proof simultaneously (walking the viewer through a leak diagnosis inside the customer's bathroom).
- Ledesma's slow burn (60+ creatives over months, ROAS climbing) and Ash Harbor's instant winner (one ad → $200K+ deal) are both real — variance is huge, so [[Creative Variation]] matters more than perfection.
- Campaign setup is "a thousand YouTube videos" away — what matters is dialing the lead form, the creative, and a willingness to test offers.
