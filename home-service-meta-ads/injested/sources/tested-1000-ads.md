[[Steve Hunsaker]] (published 2026-03-09) — 2026 update of the three-creative framework, now reflecting Meta's **Andromeda** algorithm update. Lifetime spend: $600K+ on Steve's own ad accounts plus 700+ HSA members.

## Key Claims

### Creative #1 — Owner Intro Video (30–60s)
- Owner selfie or tripod in front of branded truck/shop/customer house. Script: location → name + business → what + differentiators → CTA.
- Why it works: **exposure effect** — Meta's frequency-score mechanic re-shows the ad 2–5× before conversion. Combined with the local geographic callout, the prospect mentally relocates the advertiser from "stranger on screen" to "neighbor down the street." See [[Exposure Effect]].
- Live example: Kaden / [[Bright View Window Cleaning]] in Tucson with 600+ five-star reviews and 2025 Reader's Choice mentions baked in. Subtitles via Cap Cut beat Meta's auto-subtitles.
- Bonus example: [[Llewellyn Plumbing]]'s Provo water-softener ad — first ad Todd ever ran, printing money in days. "Genuine but not over-rehearsed" is the win.

### Creative #2 — Customer Testimonial Interview
- 99% of competitors won't get one because asking a customer is uncomfortable. That friction *is* the moat. Two paths: a brand-evangelist customer who'll co-create, or a structured interview at the customer's house with a real camera + mic + bottle of wine in exchange.
- Why it works: **social proof** + **narrative transportation** — viewers project themselves into the testimonial subject. Match the demographic to your target (Tom @ Dandy Painting filmed in mansion living rooms after joining HSA). Diversify by gender/ethnicity/income segment if your customer base is diverse.
- Live example: Valley Premier Lighting interview with the Frialey family — wife volunteers the husband-on-ladder safety angle unprompted, exactly the emotional trigger that sells.

### Creative #3 — Still Image (back, in 2026)
- ~40% of Facebook ads are watched on mute. Still image with strong visual + offer cuts through.
- Production stack: take a real photo (technician on water-softener, crew in front of truck, before/after) → feed to Manus AI / Gemini / Nano Banana Pro with logo + colors + promo + **"please ask me five clarifying questions to optimize the quality before generating."** → iterate.
- Live example: Steve's permanent-lighting campaign testing 10 still-image variants — one phone-with-app variant outperformed the rest, so future iterations tweak only colors / layout / phone position.

### Operational rules
- Minimum **5–10** creatives per campaign launch. Three is the floor. One is malpractice.
- Don't overthink camera gear — iPhone + lapel mic + $20 tripod is fine. Camera is better than what filmed Hollywood movies 30 years ago.
