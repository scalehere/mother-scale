[[Steve Hunsaker]] (published 2026-02-26). A condensed re-statement of [[Tri-Pillar Marketing System]] aimed at owners who rely on a single channel (ads only, or word of mouth only) and watch the schedule swing weekly between fully-booked and dead.

## Key Claims

- Single-channel reliance is the symptom — when one source slows, the phone goes silent and the business scrambles. The fix is meta + Google Business + physical marketing reinforcing each other.
- **Meta ads — the three biggest mistakes owners make:** (1) campaign with only 1–2 creatives, (2) targeting a 40-square-mile radius instead of the few rich pockets, (3) refusing to put the owner's face on camera. "Nut up" — humanizing the brand with a real person is non-negotiable in 2026.
- Three creative types print money: **testimonials**, **owner-facing selfie videos**, and **variations of those plus still images**. Variations means same script in different settings: in the truck, in the warehouse, with employees behind, with the owner's wife in a polo. Ledesma tested 60 creatives over 6 months for 20–25× ROAS.
- **Brand + leads in one channel:** Meta is the only paid platform that does both simultaneously. Selfie videos generate leads *and* impress brand on the local subconscious — a "digital army of virtual yous" everywhere in the market.
- **Google Business reinforces ads.** Story: a $30K customer bought because she'd been seeing the Instagram ads, then Googled the company — and her CRM record showed "came from Google." Photo reviews are extraordinarily trust-building. Action item: get **20 photo reviews** in 30 days, fill out products & services, and watch SEMrush ranking shift in 90 days. Google does *not* penalize photo reviews from out-of-state Americans (left as an aside).
- **Physical marketing multiplies results.** Story: Todd ([[Llewellyn Plumbing]]) joined HSA, ordered yard signs / A-frames / gift cards immediately, spent ~$200 on Meta and closed a $2,800 job + four estimates. Cost of change for a 6-truck operation is higher than for an owner-op — yet Todd implements faster than most one-truck operators watching free YouTube content.
- "99% of [your local competitors] don't even take action." Information isn't the moat — execution is.
