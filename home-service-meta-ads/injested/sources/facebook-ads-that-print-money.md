[[Steve Hunsaker]] (published 2025-11-10) introduces three creative blueprints that have run across his businesses ($800K in two months in temporary lighting) and the 500+ HSA member companies. Author has spent ~$600–700K on Facebook ads personally.

## Key Claims

- **Blueprint #1 — Testimonial Ads.** The hardest to produce (must convince a customer to be filmed), which is why ~99% of competitors and agencies skip them. A bottle of wine + iPhone + tripod + 3–4 ChatGPT questions is the entire production stack. The clip becomes ad creative *and* gets pinned on the homepage, Instagram, and Google Business profile. "Most of the magic you're looking for comes from the work you're avoiding" (attributed to Alex Hormozi).
- **Blueprint #2 — The "Bread and Butter" Owner Ad.** Six steps: call out neighborhood → introduce self + company → who you are / what you do → why you're different → expiring offer → audible CTA. Codified as [[Six-Step Ad Structure]]. Length 45s–1m30s. Demonstrated live with an improvised plumbing example.
- **Blueprint #3 — Still Image (revived).** No longer dead because (1) when mixed into a campaign with videos it acts as nurture across multiple touchpoints, (2) CPMs are typically lower (~$50–55 vs. ~$75 for video). Easy paths: AI-cartoonize a photo via ChatGPT then refine in Canva; before/after side-by-side with a 5-star review screenshot in the lower third.
- **Hard rule:** never publish a campaign with one creative — always 3 to 5. "It's the equivalent of putting one bullet in a revolver." The #1 reason HSA has to intervene is members publishing single-creative campaigns despite the rule.
