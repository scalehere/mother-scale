Plumbing operation in Spanish Fork / Provo, Utah. Owner: Todd. Six to eight trucks on the road. Joined [[Home Service Accelerator]] ~3–4 weeks before [[How to Get Unlimited Leads]] was filmed.

## Numbers

- ~$200 spent on Meta → closed a $2,800 job + 4 estimates queued ([[How to Get Unlimited Leads]]).
- ~$1,800 spent → ~$30K in sales weeks later ([[The $1.17M/Year Strategy]]).
- First Facebook ad ever produced is the one printing money — a water-softener pitch.

## Ad language (winning creative)

"Do you own a home in Provo, Utah? If so, please listen up for the next 30 seconds. My name's Todd, I'm the owner of Llewellyn Plumbing. And if you don't have a water softener, we'd love to come out and give you a free consultation on one. This is our main water softening unit. This is our whole home filtration unit. And this is a reverse osmosis unit so you can drink clean purified water…"

## Why Steve uses Todd as the canonical example

Higher cost of change than most viewers — 6–8 trucks, plumbers across multiple sites — yet implements yard signs / A-frames / gift cards / ads faster than the typical solo-truck owner watching the videos. The contrast is the point.
