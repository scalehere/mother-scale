Window cleaning company in Tucson, Arizona. Owner: Kaden (referenced in multiple Hunsaker sources as "Kaden the 22-year-old window cleaner running circles around 99% of competitors"). Owner is also referenced in connection with a Christmas-light side business.

## Numbers / proof

- 600+ five-star Google reviews at the time of recording.
- Won 2025 Reader's Choice Award for best window cleaner in Tucson.
- Five technicians on staff.

## Why their ads work

- Owner-intro template followed precisely: "Hey Tucson, my name is Kaden. I'm the owner of Bright View Window Cleaning…" → differentiators (reviews, awards, team) → CTA. See [[Six-Step Ad Structure]].
- Punchy ad copy: "Life's too short to have dirty windows."
- "Brightview rainlock technology" framed as a proprietary value prop (`guaranteed clean even after it rains`).
- Tested still-image variants (before/after of phone screen repair, side-by-sides) and found a winning still-image that converts → iterates with small tweaks.
