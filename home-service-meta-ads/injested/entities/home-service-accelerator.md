Paid program (sometimes called "HSA Pro") run by [[Steve Hunsaker]] that teaches home-service / contracting business owners to run their own Meta ads, optimize their Google Maps SEO, and build the back-office automation systems competitors typically pay agencies to build.

## Mechanics

- One-time fee model (vs. agency monthly retainer). Recorded onboarding call → SOPs the customer keeps forever.
- Sequenced curriculum: organic Instagram/Facebook posts → yard sign + A-frame ordering → Facebook ad filming → Cap Cut / Fiverr editing → Google Business optimization → automation systems.
- Discord community + group calls. Bounty competitions (e.g. February 2026 review-bounty: $250 + Amazon card; one member got 75 reviews in a week, another ~30–40 with photos).
- Application gate — revenue/experience minimum. Pitched as deliberately filtering out "free Facebook group" advice quality.
