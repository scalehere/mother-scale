[[Steve Hunsaker]]'s seasonal temporary holiday lighting business in the Phoenix metro / Valley of the Sun (Scottsdale, Paradise Valley, Arcadia, Gilbert, Chandler, Mesa). The single most-referenced data source in the corpus.

## Numbers

- $1.17M last year. Peak: $400–500K in a single month; $500K+ in a 2-month period; ~$800K in 2 months in another reference.
- Year 1: 95% paid ads. By Year 5: ~45% paid / 55% organic + word of mouth + physical marketing.
- Google Business: 107–108 five-star reviews despite operating only 2 months/year. 173 phone calls and 1000+ profile interactions in a season — all free.
- Peak-season Meta spend: $300–500/day.

## Why it's the canonical example

- Demonstrates the [[Tri-Pillar Marketing System]] in steady state — paid is gasoline on top of a slow-burning organic + brand fire.
- Geographic concentration: ~50% of installs in a 3–4 sq mile radius. See [[Sniper-Rifle Geographic Targeting]].
- Lead form friction: $999 minimum acknowledgement before submission filters tire-kickers.
- Average ticket: $3,800.
