Junk removal company owned by Cliff. Hunsaker's canonical case study for [[Google Maps SEO]] execution.

## Numbers

- 171 five-star Google reviews in ~12–18 months from a near-zero start.
- ~$5-figure/month from the GMB listing alone (free traffic).
- Average rank on competitive keywords ("junk removal service" 2.3, "residential junk hauling" 2.29, "same day junk pickup" near top) across the service area in semrush-style ranking grids.

## Why he ranks

- Frequency — getting reviews almost every day, not in batches.
- Photo reviews — "ugly as hell" but Google treats them as the most-relevant. See [[Photo Reviews]].
- Veteran-owned tag, synced website, owner posts (hot tub removal videos), full products & services, physical pin / address.
- On-site review nurture system + automated SMS follow-up via [[Snipy Lead]].
