Marketing agency targeting home-service contractors — websites, SEO, Google Ads. Notably **does not run Facebook ads**; the 2025 guide they published positions Facebook as a complement to website + SEO + Google Ads rather than a service line.

Domain: hookagency.com. Author of the corpus's only external source: [[Mandelyn Miller]]'s "Home Services Facebook Ads Guide 2025."

## Editorial stance vs. [[Steve Hunsaker]]

- More agency-positive on managed services; recommends hiring help when an owner hits a juggling ceiling.
- Recommends boosting posts for visibility (Steve dismisses post-boosting in favor of always running campaigns through Ads Manager).
- Both agree on: short lead forms, video > static, geo-tight targeting, avoiding stock photos, fast follow-up, [[Meta Pixel]] + [[Conversion API]] post-iOS.
