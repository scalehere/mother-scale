Holding company for Facebook and Instagram. Across the corpus, Steve uses "Facebook ads" and "Meta ads" interchangeably — Ads Manager places creative across both surfaces by default. ~190M weekly active US Facebook users, ~169M weekly active US Instagram users (Hunsaker's last-checked data).

## Algorithm shifts referenced

- **iOS opt-out (~2021):** Apple required users to opt in to tracking, breaking Meta's third-party data pipe. Meta rebuilt around creative-driven targeting. See [[Creative Beats Targeting]].
- **Andromeda update (2026):** Newest algorithm pass. Treats broad targeting + creative variation as the optimization surface; demotes manual interest/age/income narrowing.

## Surfaces & products referenced

- **Lead form ads (instant forms):** the recommended primary conversion event for home services. Custom intro, multiple-choice friction questions, contact fields.
- **[[Advantage+ Placements]]:** automated cross-surface delivery; Steve recommends leaving on for first campaigns.
- **[[Meta Pixel & Conversion API]]:** post-iOS first-party tracking layer.
- **Multi-advertiser ads:** the "cluster of 4 ads side-by-side" surface — Steve disables.

## Frequency, fatigue, scale

- Frequency score climbs as audience pool exhausts; counter with [[Creative Variation]].
- At $300–500/day in a metro, Steve hits frequency 4–5× and starts harvesting brand mindshare beyond direct conversions.
