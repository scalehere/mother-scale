The AI image-generation stack Hunsaker recommends for producing [[Still Image Ads]]. He cites Manus AI, Gemini, and Nano Banana Pro interchangeably — all in the "give it a real photo + brand colors + promo and ask for variations" workflow.

## The signature prompt

> *"Please ask me five clarifying questions to optimize the quality of your results before generating them for me."*

Forces the model to extract context up-front and ships substantially better results than blind generation. Final cleanup happens in Canva (text overlays for cases where the AI misspells words or numbers).
