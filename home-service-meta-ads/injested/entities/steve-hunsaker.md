Founder of [[Home Service Accelerator]] and operator of two home-service businesses — [[Valley Christmas Lights]] (seasonal, peaks $400–500K/month) and [[Valley Premier Lighting]] (permanent lighting). Author of 10 of the 11 sources in this corpus.

## Profile

- Started in junk removal ~5 years before recording the corpus; documented in YouTube playlist "first video … excited about $300 profit days." Now $1.17M/year in lighting alone.
- Total Facebook ad spend across his accounts: $600K+ personal, $700K+ implemented through HSA members.
- Home Service Accelerator: ~500–800 home-service / contracting businesses through the program over its 1.5-year life as the corpus was recorded (the count grows across the videos: 500 → 550 → 600 → 700 → 800).

## Content thesis

- Owners can run Meta ads in-house without agencies. Run Google PPC through an agency. See [[Google Ads vs Facebook Ads]].
- Lead-gen alone misses the point — Meta's killer feature for local businesses is doing brand-build *and* lead-gen in one channel. See [[Brand-Build + Lead-Gen Dual Purpose]].
- Repeated rhetorical move: "you're not special, you run a home service business" — implementation, not information, is the moat.
