Family-owned remodeling company in Gilbert / East Valley, Arizona. Brothers run the business; appear personally on every ad. The most-referenced HSA case study across the corpus.

## Numbers

- Spent $11K on a single ad → returned $400K+ ([[How to Run Facebook Ads]]).
- Tested 60–70 creatives over ~6 months. Reached 20–25× ROAS in peak months ([[$250K In 60 Days]], [[How to Get Unlimited Leads]]).
- Slow-burn ROAS curve: 5–7× early → climbed steadily as creatives compounded.

## Why their ads work

- Visual map pop-up of "Today we're in Gilbert" on every video — instantly converts Facebook from "stranger ad" to "neighbor."
- Stack [[Educate-Entertain-Social-Proof Framework]] in the same clip — walk through the leak diagnosis (educate) inside the customer's bathroom (social proof).
- Live customer interview cut into the ad ("This is what our clients Hillary and Gilbert had to say…") — see [[Testimonial Ad]].
- Branded uniform + branded vehicle in every shot.

## Key offer language

"East Valley homeowners choose us for one reason — we make remodeling easy. … 3D renderings, clear communication, clean job sites, premium craftsmanship. … schedule your consultation below and my brother and I will personally come out for a free design consultation."
