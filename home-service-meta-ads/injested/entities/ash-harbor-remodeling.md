Premier remodeling company in Ann Arbor, Michigan. Owners: Ash Wilke and Juliette Wilke (husband + wife). The corpus's outlier counter-example to [[Ledesma Remodeling]]'s slow-burn curve.

## The story

- Ash was reluctant to be on camera. The moment he filmed his first ad, the company sold a six-figure remodel from that single creative.
- Steve uses the pair (Ledesma slow-burn vs. Ash Harbor instant winner) to make the point that variance is huge — owners shouldn't read either outcome as the norm.
