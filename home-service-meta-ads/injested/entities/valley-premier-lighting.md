[[Steve Hunsaker]]'s permanent / year-round LED roof line lighting business — the newer counterpart to [[Valley Christmas Lights]]. Sells "smart track lighting" with a mobile app, color customization, and security uplighting.

## Positioning

- Cross-sells the seasonal Christmas customers in summer with messaging like *"Switch over to our permanent smart track lighting system and never have to hang Christmas lights again."*
- Off-season test campaigns (Feb–March) that look unprofitable for weeks because demand is non-existent — closing one $5K job converts the campaign to ~3.5× ROAS overnight. See [[High-Ticket Sales Process Patience]].
- Used by Steve as the "advanced creative testing" account: 20 simultaneous still-image + video creatives, AI-generated still images, AI-narrated videos, all running in parallel with [[Andromeda]]-era broad targeting.
