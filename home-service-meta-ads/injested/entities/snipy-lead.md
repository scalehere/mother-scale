CRM / sales automation tool referenced repeatedly as the back-office system that makes Meta lead-form campaigns work. Brand spelling varies in transcripts ("Snipy Lead", "Snip Lead", "Sniply Lead") but functionally one product.

## Use cases referenced

- **Speed to lead:** auto-call attempt the moment a lead form submits → if no answer → voicemail drop + SMS → 5 follow-up SMS over the next day → email nurture. See [[Speed to Lead Automation]].
- **Pipeline tracking:** stages from inbound lead → quote scheduled → quote given → quote approved → won, surfaced as close-rate in real time.
- **Review nurture / dead-lead recovery:** SMS sequences to request reviews after a job; phone-setter (e.g. "Lily" at Valley Premier Lighting) calls month-old non-converted leads with new offers.

Functionally analogous to GoHighLevel — both are CRM + SMS + email + voicemail-drop automation stacks marketed at home-service operators. The corpus does not mention GoHighLevel directly.
