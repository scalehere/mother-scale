Three distinct Google products show up in the corpus — easy to conflate, important to keep separate.

- **Google Business Profile / Maps:** free local pack. The cornerstone of [[Tri-Pillar Marketing System]]'s organic pillar. Ranking factors emphasized: physical pin (service-area-only loses), review **frequency**, **photo reviews**, synced socials, owner posts, fully-populated products & services, response to bad reviews. See [[Google Maps SEO]] · [[Photo Reviews]].
- **Google Pay-Per-Click (PPC):** auction-based search ads. Highest-intent paid channel. Expensive, very competitive, requires daily management — Steve recommends outsourcing to a $2K–$4K/month agency, **not** running in-house unless the business is doing 8 figures. Avoid per-lead-pricing agencies; prefer retainer (+ % of spend if available). See [[Google Ads vs Facebook Ads]].
- **Google LSA / Local Service Ads / "Guaranteed":** pay-per-call, badge-based. Mentioned only as out-of-scope ("we'll cover LSAs another time").

Note: Google does not penalize Google Business reviews left by Americans outside the service-area state — Steve mentions this without endorsing the implication, then moves on.
