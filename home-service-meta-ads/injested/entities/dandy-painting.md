Painting company owned by Tom. Cited in [[I Tested 1,000+ Facebook Ads]] as the canonical example of demographic / setting match in [[Testimonial Ad]] production.

## Why he's the example

After joining [[Home Service Accelerator]], Tom started filming all his testimonial ads in **massive mansion living rooms** (matching his target high-ticket residential customer) instead of average houses. Ad campaigns exploded. Demonstrates [[Narrative Transportation]] — viewers project into the testimonial subject's home only when the home matches their aspiration.
