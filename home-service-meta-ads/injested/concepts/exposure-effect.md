Basic psychological principle Hunsaker invokes to explain why repeated Meta impressions convert prospects who didn't react the first time.

## The mechanism

Meta's frequency score climbs as the same prospect sees an ad multiple times. Most operators panic when frequency hits 4–5× ("audience fatigued!") — but in a small geographic service area that's *exactly* the goal. By touch #3 or #4 the prospect mentally relocates the advertiser from "stranger" to "neighbor I've seen around."

## The Coca-Cola comparison

Coca-Cola doesn't run constant Facebook ads because they've achieved exposure-effect saturation through every restaurant, sporting event, gas station fridge over decades. Local home-service businesses can manufacture a miniature version of that same saturation in a 3–4 sq mile radius.

## Why it doesn't work at $50/day in a 7M-person metro

Spend has to concentrate on a small enough area to reach the same humans repeatedly. Hence [[Sniper-Rifle Geographic Targeting]] is upstream — without geographic concentration, frequency never climbs and exposure effect never kicks in.
