The slow-burning organic engine of [[Tri-Pillar Marketing System]]. The corpus has multiple worked examples of properly-optimized Google Business profiles producing high-three-figures to low-five-figures of free monthly business — entirely without paid spend.

## Non-negotiables for ranking

1. **Physical pin / address.** Service-area-only businesses lose. Find a way to get an address — even a small office.
2. **Frequency of reviews.** *Drip* reviews, don't batch them. If you have a backlog, ask customers to leave them on different days.
3. **[[Photo Reviews]].** Highest-weight review type. Geo-tagged iPhone metadata from inside the service area is what Google's "most relevant" sort optimizes for.
4. **Synced socials.** Connect Facebook + Instagram to the GBP.
5. **Owner posts.** Hot-tub-removal videos, install photos, before-and-afters.
6. **Products & services section** — fill out every conceivable service keyword.
7. **Respond to bad reviews calmly.** Don't argue with idiots; the response is read by *future* customers, not the reviewer.
8. **Don't reply to fake reviews if you intend to dispute them** — replying tanks Google's removal odds.

## Hunsaker's challenge

Get **20 photo reviews** in the next 30 days. SEMrush ranking will visibly shift in 90 days. [[Clifford's Junk Removal]] turned a near-zero start into 171 photo reviews and #1–4 rank across all competitive keywords using exactly this checklist.
