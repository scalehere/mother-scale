Meta's automated placement system that decides which surface (Facebook Feed, Instagram Feed, Reels, Stories, Marketplace, Audience Network, Messenger) shows your ad. ~50 placement options total.

## Recommendation

- **Leave Advantage+ on for first campaigns.** Even with it on, ~90% of spend lands on the high-performance placements anyway: Instagram Reels, Instagram Feed, Facebook Reels, Facebook Feed, plus a sliver of Stories.
- Only consider turning it off after a campaign has run for weeks and you've found low-quality leads coming from one specific placement (rare).
- Most agencies obsessing over manual placement choices ("Instagram Reels only!") are adding zero value vs. the automated system.

## Why operators are tempted to over-control

- Old habits from pre-Andromeda Meta where placements mattered more.
- Cheap agencies use placement-tinkering as visible "work" to justify retainers.
