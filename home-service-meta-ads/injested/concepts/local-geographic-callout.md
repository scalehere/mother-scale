Step 1 of the [[Six-Step Ad Structure]]: open the ad with the city, neighborhood, or county name. The single highest-leverage opening line in home-service Meta ads.

## What it does

Subconsciously relocates the advertiser from "stranger pitching me" to "neighbor talking to me" within the first 1–2 seconds — before the prospect's scroll instinct kicks in.

## Variants observed in the corpus

- *"What's up Scottsdale homeowners?"* (generic)
- *"East Valley homeowners choose us for one reason…"* (regional naming — "East Valley" of Arizona spans Gilbert, Chandler, Mesa; using the regional vernacular signals deeper local roots than naming a single city)
- *"Hey Tucson, my name is Kaden…"* ([[Bright View Window Cleaning]])
- *"Do you own a home in Provo, Utah? If so, please listen up for the next 30 seconds."* ([[Llewellyn Plumbing]] — interrogative form combined with urgency)
- *"What's up Ann Arbor homeowners? Is your home stuck living in the past…"* ([[Ash Harbor Remodeling]])

## Visual variant

Once the operator has run enough campaigns, the audible callout can be replaced or reinforced with on-screen text. See [[Visual Hook]].

## Why this hook can't be skipped

[[Creative Beats Targeting]] means location targeting in Ads Manager is a weaker signal than the words you say in the first second. Meta is showing your ad to people in your geographic radius, but the *creative* itself confirms the locality and triggers the trust frame.
