[[Steve Hunsaker]]'s teaching analogy for the [[Tri-Pillar Marketing System]]: building a campfire while camping.

- **Fire pit = brand.** The container — vehicle wraps, branded uniforms, on-time arrivals, an iPad for quotes instead of pen and paper. Without it nothing else holds together; with it even smoldering coals look like a real business.
- **Slow-burning log = organic.** Google Maps SEO, referral systems, review nurturers. Hard to ignite, but once lit it's hard to put out. Not scalable on demand — you can't turn organic to 1000% tomorrow.
- **Gasoline = paid ads.** Lights fast, scales fast, but burns out the moment you stop pouring. Pour gas only on a fire pit + log; pouring gas alone *is* the [[Agency Hamster Wheel]].

## The point

Pour gasoline on a slow-burning log and the fire grows brighter and bigger than gasoline alone could produce — and the log itself burns hotter. That's the compounding case for running paid + organic + physical together rather than relying on any one.
