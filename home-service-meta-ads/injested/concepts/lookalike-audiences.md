Custom audiences built from a seed list of past customers, CRM leads, or website visitors (via [[Meta Pixel]]). Meta then finds new users sharing traits (location, interests, behavior) with the seed.

## Setup (per [[Hook Agency]])

- Seed list: at least 100 people, ideally 1,000–5,000 — past customers, CRM exports, website visitors.
- The more *specific* the source audience, the better the result. A list of $5K-ticket converted customers will lookalike differently from a list of every newsletter signup.

## Where it fits in the corpus

- More prominent in [[Hook Agency]]'s playbook than Hunsaker's. Hunsaker barely mentions lookalikes — his thesis is that broad geographic targeting + creative does the job better in 2026, post-[[Creative Beats Targeting]].
- Most useful when you have a clean CRM export of best customers and want to scale outside the immediate radius.

## Practical caveat

Meta ad accounts must hit minimum size thresholds before lookalikes can be created (~100-person seed minimum). Brand-new businesses can't use this feature until they've accumulated some customer data.
