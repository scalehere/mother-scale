The single biggest psychological trigger in home-service marketing. Why referrals close at 2–3× the rate of cold leads, why testimonial ads convert, why Google photo reviews pull people in.

## Where it shows up across the corpus

- **In ads:** customer-interview testimonials, screenshots of 5-star Google reviews, iMessage screenshots from customers (names blocked), customer's home in the background of an owner-talking ad. See [[Educate-Entertain-Social-Proof Framework]].
- **In organic:** [[Photo Reviews]] on Google Business — an entry buyer can't easily fake at scale. [[Clifford's Junk Removal]]'s 171-review run.
- **In physical marketing:** yard signs at the customer's house show the dog-walking neighbor that someone else paid you. Postcards mention "we did the home down the street."

## Why home-service buyers respond especially strongly

The risk of hiring a stranger to enter your home, deal with electricity / plumbing / roofs, take a five-figure deposit, and not finish the job is real. Social proof is the only universal mechanism for evaporating that risk before the operator even gets to the on-site quote.
