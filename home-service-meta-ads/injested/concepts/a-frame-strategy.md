Branded A-frame placard placed at the bumper of the work truck during a job, *partially in the road* — designed to look like a slow-down-traffic sign. Functionally it forces drivers passing the job site to register the brand and CTA.

## Why it outperforms a vehicle wrap alone

- Vehicle wraps are heavy on **branding** but light on **call-to-action**.
- A-frames go heavy on CTA: logo + service + "Install in progress" + QR code to book a free estimate.
- Wraps + A-frames together are the combo HSA prescribes for every member.

## Bonus deployment: neighborhood entry

Place a second A-frame at the entrance of the neighborhood while the truck is on a job inside. Pull it when leaving. Captures every car driving in or out for the duration of the job — far higher impression density than the truck-side placement alone.

## Production

Same vendor + design constraints as a yard sign. Logo top, single-line service description, simple QR or "Book your free estimate" CTA. Generic / unbranded versions exist but the A-frame is almost always branded.
