The original three-pillar framework Hunsaker uses to categorize *any* high-converting home-service video ad. Distinct from (and predates) the later [[Owner-Facing Selfie Video Ad]] / [[Testimonial Ad]] / [[Still Image Ads]] taxonomy. Best ads stack 2 or 3 of these in the same clip.

## The three pillars

1. **Educate.** Walk the viewer through what's happening on a job in a way that positions the operator as an expert. Example: [[Ledesma Remodeling]] diagnosing a shower leak inside a customer's bathroom — "we see leaking at the ground, we think there's a chance it's coming from behind the wall too." The viewer subconsciously concludes *they're an expert*; therefore *I want to hire an expert*.
2. **Entertain.** Visual satisfaction. Suds scrubbing off windows, time-lapses of pressure washing, frame-to-finish pool builds, before/after transformations.
3. **Social proof.** Testimonials, paid customers visible mid-job, screenshots of 5-star Google reviews, customer iMessages with names blocked.

## Why this matters

- Owners who know the framework can analyze any ad in any niche and identify *why* it converts — and clone the structure for their own trade.
- The Ledesma bathroom-remodel ads stack educate + social proof in 60 seconds without any conscious testimonial cut, because the work is being done for an actual paying client whose home is the set.
