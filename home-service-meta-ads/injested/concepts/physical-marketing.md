The third pillar of [[Tri-Pillar Marketing System]] — the in-the-real-world layer that converts paid leads into compounding organic spread on every job.

## Components

- **[[Yard Sign Strategy]]** — branded sign in customer's yard.
- **[[A-Frame Strategy]]** — placard at truck bumper + neighborhood entry.
- **[[Gift Card Referral]]** — gift card with "gifted by [name]" + steakhouse reward to the referrer.
- **Vehicle wraps** — heavy on branding (less on CTA — A-frames complement).
- **Postcard mailers** (e.g. via [[Dope Marketing]]) — direct mail to addresses in the service zone.
- **Branded uniforms** — Nike polos for sales reps minimum; matching t-shirts for install crews.

## Why every paid lead is also a physical-marketing event

A Meta-sourced job is also an opportunity to plant a yard sign, deploy an A-frame, hand out gift cards, and seed neighborhood awareness. The *paid* spend opens the door; the *physical* infrastructure compounds it. Absent physical marketing, paid spend produces a one-time customer; with it, paid spend buys a customer plus 1–3 neighbor referrals.

## The systems test

Hunsaker's filter for whether physical marketing is actually deployed: are signs, A-frames, gift cards happening on **every job, every customer, every time** — or only when the operator is in the mood? Systems-not-mood is what separates compounding businesses from stuck ones. [[Llewellyn Plumbing]] is the canonical example of a multi-truck operation that systematized this fast.
