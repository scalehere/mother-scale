The 2026 production pipeline for [[Still Image Ads]] using AI image generators ([[Manus AI / Gemini / Nano Banana Pro]]) plus Canva for cleanup.

## The pipeline

1. **Source photo.** Real photo of a technician with hand on equipment, crew in front of branded truck, before/after, finished install. The AI builds *on top of* this image rather than fabricating from scratch.
2. **Prompt with brand assets.** Logo file, brand colors, current promo dollar amount, target dimensions for Facebook lead-form image.
3. **Force the model to question first.** Append: *"Please ask me five clarifying questions to optimize the quality of your results before generating them for me."* This consistently produces better output than blind generation — the model surfaces ambiguity (e.g. "should the discount text be top or bottom?").
4. **Iterate on a winning seed photo.** Provide a second photo (truck shot), then a third (before/after side-by-side) — collect 2–4 distinct AI variants per source.
5. **Canva for text correction.** AI image models routinely garble text and numbers. Take the AI image into Canva and overlay correct copy.

## Where it slots into the broader workflow

- AI still images are *one* of three creative types in a Meta campaign — never a substitute for [[Owner-Facing Selfie Video Ad]] / [[Testimonial Ad]].
- Hunsaker explicitly warns operators: don't use AI as an excuse to skip filming yourself. He's an experienced ad-buyer testing AI for incremental ROAS — beginners who haven't run owner-facing video shouldn't lean on AI ads as their primary creative.
