Meta's defining property as an ad channel — and the deepest-running difference between Facebook/Instagram and Google PPC.

## Definition

- **Interruption-style (Facebook, Instagram, TikTok, TV):** the prospect is not searching for you. They're watching cat videos / scrolling reels / consuming sports highlights when your ad pops up. You convince them to take action.
- **Intent-based (Google PPC, LSAs):** the prospect typed *"plumber near me"* and is actively looking. You convince them you're the best option.

## Strategic consequences

- **Interruption requires creative.** What you say + how you say it determines whether anyone converts. Targeting is gutted (see [[Creative Beats Targeting]]); creative is the lever.
- **Intent requires bidding skill.** Targeting still matters — keyword choices, negative keywords, audience refinement. The creative is much less important; the visibility is the game.
- **Interruption is cheap per impression but lower-intent.** Intent is expensive per click but higher-intent.
- **Brand-build is essentially a Meta-only superpower** — interruption ads put your face in front of the prospect 3–5× before they convert (see [[Exposure Effect]]), so even non-converting impressions plant brand. Google PPC only triggers on active demand and offers no equivalent compounding.

## How to apply

When advising channel mix: ask whether the trade has predictable search demand (plumbing emergencies, roof leaks, AC outages) or whether demand has to be created (remodeling, permanent lighting, pool builds). Search-driven trades over-index Google. Demand-creation trades over-index Meta.
