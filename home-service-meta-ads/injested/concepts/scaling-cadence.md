The rule for raising daily Meta budget without crashing campaign performance: **15–25% per week.**

## The mechanic

- Big budget jumps (e.g. $50/day → $500/day) reset the algorithm's learning phase. The campaign loses the prior winners and starts re-discovering audiences from scratch — usually at much higher CPL.
- Small steady increases let Meta gradually expand the audience pool while staying inside the converting cohort.

## The arithmetic

- Spending $100/day comfortably → after 1 week, scale to ~$125/day.
- Following week → ~$155/day.
- After ~6 weeks → ~$300/day, more than 3× the starting budget.

## What can break this

- Scaling without adding new creatives. As budget grows, the same audience saturates faster (see [[Creative Fatigue]]). New creatives need to ship in parallel with budget increases.
- Operator panic at week-2 dip. Some weeks CPL spikes; the right action is patience, not scaling back. Steve: "you can afford to spend a little on testing before you can't pay rent."

## Counter-camp note

Some operators believe Meta no longer cares about budget jumps. Steve's experience: the rule still matters, especially in geographically-concentrated campaigns where the audience pool is small.
