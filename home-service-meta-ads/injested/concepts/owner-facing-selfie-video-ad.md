The default creative type Hunsaker prescribes for any home-service first-campaign. Owner on camera, branded uniform, branded vehicle in frame, following the [[Six-Step Ad Structure]]. 30–60 seconds with subtitles.

## Why it works

- **Humanizes the brand.** Buyers don't trust logos; they trust faces.
- **Two-purpose channel.** Generates leads (form fields) *and* builds local brand simultaneously — see [[Brand-Build + Lead-Gen Dual Purpose]].
- **Skill-gap moat.** Most agency-run accounts use AI slop or generic stock footage; the owner-facing format is hard for incumbents to copy because it requires the owner's time and discomfort. See [[Skill Gap]].

## Common mistakes

- White t-shirt with bacon stains. Casualness humanizes; sloppiness kills the booking.
- Filming once and not iterating. Rule: 4–8 creatives per launch. See [[Creative Variation]].
- Skipping the audible CTA at the end.
- Recording without a lapel mic — bad audio breaks the channel even when video is fine.
