The most common reason high-ticket trades give up on Meta ads before they work: killing the campaign before the sales cycle finishes.

## The trades affected

Remodelers, roofers, plumbers, pool builders, permanent lighting, landscape design, painting, contractors of any kind. Sales cycle is **3 weeks–3 months** because the husband-and-wife conversation happens, financing gets secured, multiple quotes get compared.

## What goes wrong

- Owner spends $300–500 in week 1, doesn't see closes, panics, kills the campaign.
- Pipeline was actually full — they just weren't past the deposit stage yet.
- Real example from [[The $1.17M/Year Strategy]]: $1,500 spent in February on permanent lighting, zero closes for 2 weeks → about to close one $5K deal that converts the campaign from negative to ~3.5× ROAS instantly.

## The discipline

- **Measure performance in weeks/months, not days.** Window cleaning gets dopamine hits in 2 days; permanent lighting needs 4–8 weeks of pipeline-cooking before close-rate stabilizes.
- **Keep filling the pipeline anyway.** Pay a phone-setter (Hunsaker calls his "Lily") to call dead leads at 30+ days — recovers 2+ extra appointments/month from already-paid spend.
- **Track CAC over the season**, not the week. Off-season campaigns may carry a 10× CPL — once peak demand returns, that pipeline closes at the normal rate.

## The micro discipline ([[$250K In 60 Days]])

> *"You have to let your sales process cook. Some people get lucky and someone writes them a check in week 1. Most need to generate 20–30 leads, nurture them, and let one or two close to make a massive difference. People kill campaigns too quickly because they were impatient."*
