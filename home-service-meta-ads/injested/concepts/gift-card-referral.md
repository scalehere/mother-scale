Branded gift cards handed to every customer at job completion — used to drive friend-and-neighbor referrals at near-zero CAC.

## The mechanic

- Each card has a fixed dollar value (e.g. $50 off a service).
- Back of the card: *"Gifted by ____"* — the rep writes the customer's name in marker before handing them over.
- The pitch to the customer: "Your name's on the back. When you give one to a friend or neighbor and they redeem it, we'll send you a $X gift card to a steakhouse."
- Customer becomes the distribution channel; their *name* on the back makes the prospect treat it as a personal endorsement, not a coupon drop.

## Why CAC is so low

- The redemption discount is roughly the cost of customer acquisition — and the steakhouse reward to the referrer is small relative to the lifetime value of a referred customer.
- Quality of lead is high because it's effectively a soft referral with a built-in offer.

## Where it slots into the system

The gift card is the closing motion of the [[Tri-Pillar Marketing System]] — every paid lead that converts gets a yard sign + an A-frame impression at the job + gift cards on exit. Three exit ramps for organic spread, each leveraging the paid spend that produced the original customer.
