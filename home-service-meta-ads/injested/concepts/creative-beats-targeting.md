The single largest strategic shift in Meta advertising over the past 4 years. Targeting settings don't matter much; what you say in the ad does the targeting.

## The cause

Apple's iOS 14 opt-out (~2021) cut off Meta's third-party tracking pipe overnight. Meta rebuilt the algorithm so the *creative* finds the audience: people who watch a permanent-lighting ad to completion train Meta to show similar ads to similar profiles. The newer **Andromeda** update doubled down on this — manual interest / age / income targeting now actively *hurts* most campaigns.

## What this means in practice

- Don't add language settings, detailed interests, narrow age bands. Let the algorithm find your audience through creative.
- The "income targeting" that appears in Ads Manager is *neighborhood* income, not individual income — and works poorly compared to broad targeting.
- Steve in 2026: hardcoding country exclusions only; otherwise broad. Demonstrated by his ads-manager screen-share in [[The $1.17M/Year Strategy]].
- The 3–5 creative rule (see [[Creative Variation]]) is the real targeting lever — Meta picks winners across creatives in 24–48 hours.

## What the agency model lost

This is also the death of the cheap-agency value prop. There is essentially nothing left to "manage" once targeting is automated. See [[Agency Hamster Wheel]].
