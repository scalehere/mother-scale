Hunsaker's term for concentrating marketing spend (Meta + organic + physical) on a small geographic radius rather than blanketing the metro. The opposite of the "shotgun approach."

## The data

[[Valley Christmas Lights]]: ~50% of all installs come from a single 3–4 sq mile radius (rich pockets of the East Valley). The other 50% scatters across hill neighborhoods, but the concentration is what makes the system compound.

## What concentration produces

- **Brand saturation.** Trucks driving the same blocks repeatedly. Yard signs visible to dog-walking neighbors. Meta frequency score climbing into 4–5× because the same audience pool keeps seeing ads (see [[Exposure Effect]] / [[Creative Fatigue]]).
- **Tighter referral graphs.** Customers refer to neighbors who refer to neighbors.
- **Lower drive time.** Crews fit 4–5 jobs/day instead of 3 because the next stop is 5 minutes away.
- **Cheaper paid CPL.** Smaller audience but higher relevance.

## Setup in Meta Ads Manager

- Locations: type the city, *shrink* the default 25-mile radius, prefer "current city only."
- **Uncheck "reach more people likely to respond"** — Meta otherwise leaks spend to outside-area prospects to maximize raw lead count.
- Don't bother with detailed interest targeting — let creative + geography filter.
