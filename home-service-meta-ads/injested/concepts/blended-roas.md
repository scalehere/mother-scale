The metric that takes over from direct ROAS once the [[Tri-Pillar Marketing System]] is running and channels start co-touching the same buyer.

## The attribution problem

A real Hunsaker example: a $30K customer's CRM record says "came from Google" — but on-site she tells the rep she's been seeing the funny Instagram ads. She probably also got a postcard mailer + saw the yard sign across the street. Meta gets zero direct credit. Direct ROAS *understates* every channel.

## The blended view

Blended ROAS = total revenue from all marketing-touched customers ÷ total marketing spend across all channels (Meta + Google PPC + Google LSA + postcards + yard signs + gift cards + Snipy Lead software).

Blended CAC = total marketing spend ÷ total new customers.

## When to switch to blended thinking

Hunsaker's test: when total monthly marketing spend across channels reaches a few hundred dollars per day, channels start cross-pollinating and direct ROAS becomes misleading. Below that threshold (single channel, $50–100/day total), direct ROAS per channel is fine.

## How to apply

If an operator is killing a Meta campaign because direct ROAS looks marginal, ask whether Google calls / website visits / yard-sign calls have lifted in the same period. Often the campaign is paying for itself through other channels — and shutting it off will tank everything.
