Static-image ads — declared dead in 2022, declared back by Hunsaker in 2026. Now the third leg of the creative stool alongside [[Owner-Facing Selfie Video Ad]] and [[Testimonial Ad]].

## Why they work in 2026

- ~40% of Facebook ads are watched on mute. A strong static visual + offer cuts through where a video without subtitles fails.
- CPMs are typically lower (~$50–55 vs. ~$75 for video).
- When mixed into a campaign with videos they act as **nurture content** — Meta serves them as touch #2 / #3 / #4 to people who didn't convert on video.

## Production playbook

- Take a real photo (technician with a hand on the equipment, crew in front of branded truck, before/after side-by-side).
- Feed to [[Manus AI / Gemini / Nano Banana Pro]] with logo, brand colors, promo, and the signature instruction: *"please ask me five clarifying questions to optimize the quality of your results before generating them."*
- If AI misspells text or numbers, take the AI image into Canva and overlay correct text.
- For trades with ugly before/after (junk removal): focus on crew and trucks, not the trash.
- Easy variant: add a 5-star Google review screenshot or iMessage screenshot in the lower third of the image.

## In-account testing pattern

Launch 10+ still-image variants, find the winner, then iterate small changes (color, layout, position of phone) on the winner. See [[Creative Variation]] and [[AI-Generated Creative Workflow]].
