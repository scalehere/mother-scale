The non-negotiable back-office layer that determines whether Meta ad campaigns produce 2–3× ROAS or 6–10× ROAS. The creative gets the lead; speed to lead converts it.

## The ideal sequence (per Hunsaker / [[Snipy Lead]])

The moment a Meta lead form submits:

1. **Auto-call attempt** within seconds. If they answer, set the in-person appointment.
2. If no answer → **voicemail drop** + **immediate SMS** ("Hey [name], this is [rep] from [business]. We left you a voicemail — feel free to reach back out.")
3. **+1 hour:** SMS "Let us know — happy to help."
4. **+1 day:** SMS "Keep us posted."
5. Email nurture in parallel.
6. The moment the prospect responds (call back or text back), automation cancels remaining touches and the rep takes over.

## Why this matters mathematically

- 100 leads with 48-hour callbacks → most go cold; campaign looks like garbage.
- Same 100 leads with 5-minute auto-touches → 6–10× ROAS routine.
- "Facebook ads don't work" almost always means "your follow-up doesn't work."

## Counter-intuitive operational point

For high-ticket trades, having Snipy Lead also do **dead-lead recovery** — paying a phone-setter to call leads from 30+ days ago — surfaces 2+ extra appointments per month that already-paid-for ads originated. See [[High-Ticket Sales Process Patience]].
