On-screen visual element used in the first 1–2 seconds of an ad to substitute for or reinforce the audible neighborhood callout in the [[Six-Step Ad Structure]].

## When to use it

- ~40% of Facebook ads watched on mute → audible-only hooks are wasted on those viewers.
- Visually compelling product or location imagery (e.g. a beautifully-lit holiday home) where the *image* is the hook and saying the city name out loud would dilute it.

## Example (Hunsaker [[Valley Christmas Lights]])

Ad opens on a daytime home shot with the city name in on-screen text. *"My name is Steve. I'm the owner of Valley Christmas Lights and I'm standing in front of a home in Arcadia that we installed this year."* Visual hook + location hook stacked — converts both muted scrollers and audio listeners.

## Operator-skill flag

Visual hooks are an *advanced* lever. Beginners should start with the audible neighborhood callout from step 1 of the [[Six-Step Ad Structure]] until they have a winning creative — then experiment with visual-hook variants.
