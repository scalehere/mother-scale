Google reviews that include a customer-uploaded photo. Hunsaker's most-emphasized GMB ranking factor — and the highest-leverage [[Social Proof]] asset for a home-service buyer doing on-the-spot research.

## Why they outweigh plain-text reviews

- Google's "most relevant" sort surfaces them first.
- Hard for a competitor to fake at scale (real iPhone metadata, real geo-tagging).
- For an active buyer, photos are the fastest way to evaluate quality without needing to read text.

## Hunsaker's claim

> *"I would rather have a business that has 50 photo reviews than a business that has 200 reviews with just text."*

## How to get them

- Ask on-site at job completion — branded review-request flow with QR code on the truck or invoice.
- Automated SMS via [[Snipy Lead]] post-job: "Hey [name], if you're happy with the install, would you snap a photo and leave us a quick review? [link]."
- Bounty-style competitions with the team — [[Home Service Accelerator]] ran one in February 2026 (member got 75 reviews in a week).

## Geographic note

Google does not penalize photo reviews from out-of-state Americans, but ideally photos come from inside the service area because the metadata reinforces locality. Hunsaker mentions this as an aside without endorsing.
