The defining strategic case for Meta ads in home services. Properly run, Meta is the *only* paid channel that simultaneously generates direct leads (form submissions) **and** builds local brand mindshare with prospects who don't yet convert.

## How the dual purpose compounds

- A profitable Meta campaign at $50–100/day produces direct leads. Owner scales to $300–500/day.
- At higher spend, the same audience pool is hit 3–5× per person (rising frequency score).
- Non-converting prospects still see the owner's face / brand / vehicle 3+ times → they Google the company later, see the loaded GMB profile, and convert under "organic Google" attribution. See [[Blended ROAS / Customer Acquisition Cost]].
- The IV-bar staff, the banker, the barber all start telling the owner "I see your ads everywhere" — direct evidence of the local saturation effect.

## What Google PPC can't do

Google PPC pays only when there's intent. No exposure to non-searchers. No ambient brand build. Search ads can grow revenue but they can't make the owner a local celebrity. Meta does both.

## Why most agencies destroy this

Cheap agencies serve still images / AI slop / non-owner content. Even if those produce direct leads, no brand-build accrues — the audience doesn't recognize a face or a brand. The dual-purpose lever requires owner-facing creative, branded uniform, branded vehicle, and the [[Six-Step Ad Structure]].
