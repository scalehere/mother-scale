The wall every Meta ad account hits at scale: the same audience keeps seeing the same creative, the frequency score climbs (4×, 5×, 6× per person), conversion rate drops, and the campaign appears to be "broken." It isn't — the audience is exhausted on those specific creatives.

## When it shows up

- Small spends ($50–100/day) in a populous metro: rarely. Audience pool is huge relative to reach.
- Higher spends ($300–500/day) in a defined service area: routinely after a few weeks on the same creative set.

## The fix

Continuously launch new creative variations. Keep the **structure** that's winning ([[Six-Step Ad Structure]]) and vary the *setting* — same script in the truck, in the warehouse, with the spouse on camera, with crew working behind. See [[Creative Variation]].

## Counter-intuitive consequence

Creative fatigue is actually a *symptom of working ads*. The same campaign at $50/day with no fatigue is reaching too few people to matter. When the frequency score is climbing into the 4–5× range, the operator is also harvesting [[Brand-Build + Lead-Gen Dual Purpose]] mindshare in their service area.
