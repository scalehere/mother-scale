Branded yard sign installed in every paying customer's yard at job completion. The single highest-leverage physical-marketing asset for converting a one-customer paid-ad spend into multiple neighbor referrals.

## The mechanic

- "Birds of a feather" effect: dog-walking neighbors see the sign, mentally credit it as social proof from someone they trust (their neighbor), then call.
- Combines with a [[Sniper-Rifle Geographic Targeting]] strategy — when 50% of jobs are in a 3–4 sq mile radius, signs cluster and the saturation effect compounds.

## Two distinct sign types

1. **Branded yard sign** in the customer's yard — needs to look pretty, since the customer has to opt in.
2. **Generic non-branded sign** in public spaces — different strategy, no customer permission needed, mostly used for neighborhood entry-point placement (similar to A-frame).

## Customer reluctance — the workaround

Some trades (window cleaning, pool service) have customers who don't want yard signs. Bribe with: $5 Starbucks gift card, X% off next service, or a free 5–10-minute upsell service. Most customers actually treat the sign as a flex once they've had the install — frame it that way.

## Removal terms

Tell the customer they can pull it whenever they mow the lawn. If they're opposed, return and pull it. The friendly removal policy is what keeps adoption high.
