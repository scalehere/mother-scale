Customer-interview style ad creative. Hunsaker calls it the highest-leverage low-cost asset most home-service businesses ignore — because asking a customer to be filmed is uncomfortable enough to filter out the lazy 99%.

## Production stack

- iPhone + lapel mic + tripod. ChatGPT-prepared 3–4 questions: *"How was the experience? Why did you choose us? Would you recommend us? What did you think of the final result?"*
- Bottle of wine in exchange for the interview. If you can't find one customer in your CRM willing to do this, "you have bigger issues in your business."
- Some operators graduate to: real videographer + $30K-customer-class interviews shot in the customer's home (matched to your target demographic — see [[Narrative Transportation]]).

## Where to deploy

- Cut into video ads or run as standalone ads.
- Pin to website homepage if there's no greeting video yet.
- Pin to Instagram and Google Business profile for organic reinforcement.

## Why it works

[[Social Proof]] + [[Narrative Transportation]]. Buyers project themselves into the testimonial subject when the subject demographically matches them — which is why [[Dandy Painting]] only films in mansions matched to its target market. Testimonial ads close at 2–3× the close rate of cold-traffic leads, because the risk of hiring a stranger evaporates.
