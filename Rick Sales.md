Mental Maps
- Map isn't the territory, its a visual representation
- When talking to contractors use their mental maps, not your own
- Get on their map, stay on their map, let go of our own map

IMPORTANT WORDS:
- why asking all these questions: prescription before diagnosis is malpractice

EXPLORING QUESTIONS:
- FIRST QUESTION - could you tell me a little but about your business? | WHERE IS YOUR PAIN, DEEPEST OPPORTUNITY
- If they say what do you want to know? --- then say --- WHAT DO YOU BELIEVE IS MOST IMPORTANT?
- FIRST QUESTION FOR ALL MEETINGS AFTER FIRST MEETING - What's changed since we last talked?

- IF YOU DON'T UNDERSTAND ANYTHING THEY SAY OR THERE IS POTENTIAL FOR MISUNDERSTANDING --- CLARIFY!
- MILLIONS DOLLAR QUESTION: "what do you mean by..." | You're clarifying and defining terms | Dissect sentences or statements
- "I've just got to figure out how to cut my costs right now." - Most sales people try to lower price and don't even know what they mean
- THIS ALL GENERATES A STORY - Clients are all going to tell a bunch of mini stories | You have to slowdown to go faster, ask these questions and be meticulate
- 80% of the population processes information visually

- THIRD TYPE OF QUESTION: Expanding - Tell me more, in what ways
- WHAT and HOW --- not who, when, where and why | Open-ended and not accusatory
- What prompts you to say that?
- We don't care what the content of the conversation, we are content free --- WE ARE PLAYING WITH THE PROCESS
- You can't react emotionally to the content, go with the process --- people love to talk to you
- "I HATE salespeople" -- What does that hate come from?
- We have a million dollar budget -- don't react emotionally
- At the end of the meeting you want to be able to draw a mental map of the entire business landscape so then you got a shot of looking at the entire landscape and figure out how to best help the client --- Get the map and get it on paper
- ONLY RECORD CONTENT IN THIS SECTION NO REACTING
- THEY'RE JUST GOING TO TELL YOU STORY AFTER STORY - BE READY
- WHATEVER THEY BRING UP IS FAIR GAME
- WHEN SOMEONE THROWS SOMETHING HARD AT YOUR REACT GENTLY
- !!!!!!!!!SUPER IMPORTANT: GET THEM TO RANK ISSUES OR GOALS!!!!!!!!!!
- MOST OR LEAST IMPORTANT HELPS YOU FORM THEIR MENTAL MAP

- 4TH TYPE OF QUESTION: IMPACT QUESTION (people are prone to skip this step, THIS STEP IS WHERE THE MONEY IS)
- Ask at the end of the questioning cycle to understand cost of the problem or value of opportunity
- Document the value of your solution
- Impact can be dollars (gained lost), time(speed up/ slow down), people(loss/gain or moral), personal impact, competitive advatage/disadvatage, process, other
- Do the math, make them give you the number
- make your cost sound super cheap and silly at the end, only works when they give you numbers
- ALL THE STORIES BEFORE ARE WHERE YOU GET ALL THE STUFF TO ASK ABOUT IN THIS STEP
- how will that impact you? what impact would it have on the organization, what would the effect of that be , how would that feel, who would be most impacted, what would the consequences be?
- how the brain works: if a brain hears a large number and subsequently hear a small number the small number seems smaller. if you hear a small number and then hear a big number it seems even bigger.
- YOU NEED TO ESTABLISH THE VALUE OF THE SOLUTION THEY WANT (their vision for the future)
- What does the business look like now and what do you want it to look like in the future. Whats your VISION for the future? You told me last year you had..... what do you want this year to look like?
- !!!Where are you now and where do you want to be. If you where there what impact would it have?!!!

- 5TH TYPE OF QUESTION:
- Then you ask: What is your plan? What are you going to do? etc... PLAN/DO/HANDLE Questions | Make them ask for your help, were asking questions because they don't want to hear us talk
- THEN IS YOUR INVITATION TO COLLABORATION AS PARTNERS | The minute they ask for your help then the door is wide open, you persuade people 97% of the time with questions
- You don't want interpersonal tension you want to increase "need" tension -- Get them engaged in the opportunity.
