## SCRIPT 1 — THE CORE AD (Generic, 60 seconds)

**Setting:** Shoot on-site at Victor's job in Carlsbad. Hard hat or just the active site behind you.

**[Hook — look straight at camera, no smile yet, just direct]**
"If you're a contractor in San Diego and you want more jobs, keep watching."

**[Problem]**
"Most contractors I talk to have the same problem. They're either relying on word of mouth, or they're buying leads from some aggregator where the same lead went to 4 other guys before it got to them. So by the time you call, the customer already booked someone else."

**[Credibility — gesture to site or walk slightly]**
"I'm standing on a job site right now in Carlsbad. This contractor is one of our clients. In the last 12 weeks, we got him 90 qualified leads. Exclusive. Nobody else touched them."

**[Offer]**
"Here's what we do at ScaleSD. We run your ads, we film your content, we edit it, we manage everything. You don't touch any of it. You just close the jobs."

**[Guarantee]**
"And we guarantee 90 qualified leads in 12 weeks. If we don't hit it, you don't pay."

**[CTA]**
"If you're a contractor in San Diego, roofing, HVAC, pools, general contracting, tap the link below. Fill out a quick form and we'll reach out within the day."

## SCRIPT 2 — THE PAIN-POINT AD (60 seconds)

**[Hook]**
"Real talk. Why is your phone slow right now?"

**[Pain points — deliver these like you're listing facts, not selling]**
"It's not your work. You're good at what you do. The problem is nobody knows you exist outside of your last customer's circle. You're posting on Facebook and getting nothing. You tried Google ads and burned through $2,000 with two calls to show for it. Or you're buying leads from Angi or Thumbtack and competing with 5 other companies for the same person."

**[Pivot]**
"That whole system is broken. And it's not your fault. You weren't trained in marketing, you were trained to do the work."

**[Solution]**
"ScaleSD handles the whole thing. We film the ads, actually on your job site, like this one, we run them, we manage the leads. You just show up and close."

**[CTA]**
"Link below. Two-minute form. We'll tell you exactly what we'd do for your business."
