# Scale SD — Product Spec for Ad-Writing

> What we sell. Loaded BEFORE every ad script. Source of truth for product claims.

## What Scale SD actually does (one paragraph)

Scale SD is a contractor growth system. We film ad creative on the contractor's actual job site, edit it, run it on Meta and TikTok, manage the leads, and route the qualified ones into the contractor's calendar. The contractor doesn't touch the marketing. They show up to the job site, run their crew, and close the calls we send them.

## The wedge (our differentiator, locked)

We shoot LOCALLY with a videographer. The contractor's face, the contractor's worksite, the contractor's real projects show up in the ads. Not stock footage. Not banner ads. Not lead-vendor templates.

**Why this wins:** modern contractors need to be on camera. Credibility and trust drive lead quality now. Banner ads and Angi-style shared leads are the old game.
