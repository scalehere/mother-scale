# Angle 05 — "The Only Problem Will Be the Volume"

> Source: Spark ad 861897393207051. Running 145+ days. 21-second video. Top performer.
> Pattern: curiosity hook + abundance painting + soft CTA.
> Why this works: contractors are tired of "you'll get more jobs" copy. This flips it: "the issue is too many." Different.

## Original (Spark)

"The only problem you'll have is dealing with all the leads."

## Default rewrite — GC, EN, 20s, Peter on-camera

**[Hook]**
"The only problem you're going to have is figuring out which jobs to take."

**[Body]**
"That's not a sales line. That's literally what one of our clients told me last week. Phone moving, calendar filling, and now he has to pick. We get contractors that problem."

**[Offer + guarantee]**
"90 exclusive leads in 12 weeks. Yours alone, never shared. If we don't hit it, you don't pay."

**[CTA]**
"Tap below or DM us 'SCALE'."
