# Angle 07 — "Burned by Agencies / Tired of Promises"

> Source: Spark ads 998533309281671, 2006772290049397 ("Running ads without real results...")
> Pattern: previously-burned reframe. Targets contractors who've already paid $2k+ to a marketing agency and got nothing.
> Why this matters: this is HALF our future client base. Every contractor over $200k revenue has been burned at least once.

## Original (Spark)

"Remodelers, Want More Projects From Your Ads? Running ads without real results can waste time, budget, and slow your remodeling business growth."

## Default rewrite — GC, EN, 45s, Peter on-camera

**[Hook]**
"You already paid an agency to run ads. They didn't work."

**[Body]**
"You're not alone. Half the contractors I talk to in San Diego had a marketing agency before us. They spent two grand a month for six months. Got reports full of clicks. Didn't get a single job. So they fired the agency and went back to referrals."

**[Pivot]**
"The reason ads didn't work isn't ads. It's that the agency wasn't on your job site. They never met you. They ran stock-template campaigns that didn't show your work."

**[Solution + guarantee]**
"Different deal here. We come to your site. We film your real work. We run those ads, not stock ones. 90 exclusive leads in 12 weeks. Yours alone, never shared. If we don't hit it, you don't pay."

**[CTA]**
"Tap below."
