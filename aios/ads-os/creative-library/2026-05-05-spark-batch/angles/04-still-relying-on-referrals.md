# Angle 04 — "Still Relying on Referrals?"

> Source: Spark ads 1935852100377792, 1712186009768900 ("Still Relying Only on Referrals?")
> Pattern: pain-led question. Named the thing that's true for 80% of contractors.
> Why this is a winner: every contractor we talk to says some version of "we get most of our work from referrals." This angle attacks the dependency.

## Original (Spark)

"Remodelers, Still Relying Only on Referrals? Depending on word of mouth alone can slow down your remodeling business growth."

## Default rewrite — GC, EN, 30s, Peter on-camera

**[Hook]**
"How many of your jobs came from referrals last year?"

**[Body]**
"If the number's high, that's a compliment to your work. It's also a problem. Referrals don't fire when the calendar's empty. They don't show up on a Monday in February. They show up when somebody else's project finishes."

**[Offer + guarantee]**
"We give contractors a second source. Exclusive leads from ads we run on Instagram and Facebook. Yours alone. 90 in 12 weeks."

**[CTA]**
"Tap below."
