# Angle 01 — "Good Work, Broken System"

> Source: Spark ad 843472174969820. Running 140+ days. 5-second video. Highest-confidence winner.
> Pattern: ego-validation + scapegoat. Tells contractor their craft is fine, the system is the problem.
> Why this works: contractors don't like being told they're bad at their job. This angle never accuses them.

## Original (Spark, paraphrase from Manus headline)

"A lot of contractors aren't failing because of their work... they're failing because their marketing system is broken."

## Default rewrite — GC, EN, 30s, Peter on-camera at Victor's job site

**[Hook, 0-3s — direct to lens, no smile, gesture to site behind you]**
"Your work isn't the problem."

**[Body, 3-22s]**
"I talk to contractors every week who do great work. Photos look clean. Reviews are five stars. And the phone still doesn't ring like it should. The work isn't broken. The system that gets people to call you is broken."

**[Offer + guarantee, 22-28s]**
"We fix that part. We film, we run the ads, we manage the leads. 90 exclusive leads in 12 weeks. Yours alone, never shared."

**[CTA, 28-30s]**
"Tap below."
