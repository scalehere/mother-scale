# Angle 06 — "Marketing Is Investment, Not Expense"

> Source: Spark ad 806209695590325 ("Thinking Marketing Is Just an Expense?")
> Pattern: cost-reframe. Targets contractors who think ads are a luxury.

## Original (Spark)

"Contractors: Thinking Marketing Is Just an Expense? Most contractors don't realize the real cost is NOT having marketing."

**[Hook]**
"Stop thinking about marketing as a cost."

**[Body]**
"Most contractors I talk to do the math wrong. They look at $1,500 a month and say 'that's expensive.' They don't look at the $50,000 job they didn't book because nobody local knew their name. The expense isn't running ads. The expense is being invisible."

**[Offer + guarantee]**
"We make you visible. We film, we run, we manage. 90 exclusive leads in 12 weeks. Yours alone."

**[CTA]**
"Tap below."
