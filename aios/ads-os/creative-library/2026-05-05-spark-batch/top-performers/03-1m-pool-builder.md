# Top Performer 03 — "$1M for Pool Builder"

> Source: Spark ad 1230254495841666. Running 75+ days. 17s video.
> Why it wins: niche specificity (pool) + geo (California) + massive number.
> **Critical risk:** $1M claim not ours to make. All variations swap the dollar anchor.

## Original (Spark)

17s. "Over $1,000,000 in projects from our marketing services for a pool builder here in California."

## 10 variations

### V1 — Pool anchor, no dollar (15s, Pool)
"One San Diego pool builder. We filmed his builds, ran the ads, sent him exclusive leads. He's not chasing work anymore. He's choosing it."

### V2 — Tony reference (verbal, indirect) (15s, Pool)
"We work with a pool contractor in San Diego. Last week he told me he can't take more jobs without hiring. That's the problem we want pool guys to have."

### V3 — Niche callout, GC (15s)
"One general contractor in Carlsbad. Real builds. Real ads. Now real calendar problems."

### V4 — Outcome only, HVAC (15s)
"One HVAC contractor went from depending on shared leads to pre-booking installs in March."

### V5 — Outcome only, Roofing (15s)
"One San Diego roofer went from chasing inspection-only leads to a full install schedule. Same crew. Different marketing."

### V6 — Spanish (15s, ES, Pool)
"Un constructor de albercas en San Diego. Filmamos sus instalaciones. Corrimos los anuncios. Le mandamos leads exclusivos. Ahora elige los trabajos."

### V7 — Trade switch, Plumbing (15s)
"One San Diego plumber stopped taking drain calls because his repipe schedule is locked out. We helped him get there."

### V8 — Specific time-frame (20s, GC)
"Three months ago, this contractor's pipeline was empty. Today his crew is fully scheduled through summer. We didn't change his pricing. We changed who sees him."

### V9 — Visual-led (15s, Pool, on a finished pool)
*Peter standing at a finished pool:* "This pool builder we work with says ad leads converted three times better than referrals. That's the difference."

### V10 — Founder testimonial intro (10s, Tony if filmed separately)
*Tony to camera:* "Before ScaleSD, I'd hope for the next call. Now I'm picking which to take." *Cut to Peter:* "That's how you know it's working."
