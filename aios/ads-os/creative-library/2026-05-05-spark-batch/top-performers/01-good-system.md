**Why it works (Manus hypothesis):** Validates the contractor's ego (they do good work) while identifying an external enemy (a broken marketing system). Shifts blame from craftsmanship to system. Highly palatable.

**Pattern to extract for our rewrites:**
1. Open with empathy ("not your fault" / "your work isn't the problem")
2. Name the external enemy ("system is broken")
3. Promise the dream-state ("predictable revenue, full schedule")
4. Reframe success ("luck → normal")
5. DM CTA, conversational

**Note:** Cesar uses a long body for a 5s ad — most words are likely on-screen text, NOT spoken. He probably says only the hook + CTA, with the body running as text overlays. Mirror this technique: speak ~10-12 words, overlay 30+ words.
