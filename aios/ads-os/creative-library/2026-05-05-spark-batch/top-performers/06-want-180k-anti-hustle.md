# Top Performer 06 — "Want to Hit $180K?" Anti-Hustle (Path B addition)

> Source: Spark ad 3344252032422710. Verbatim from Manus v2. Recent ad with strong specificity.
> Why it wins: Manus called the "specificity of numbers ($180K, 69 leads, $24 CPL)" the credibility-builder. Attacks hustle-culture mentality directly.

```
✅ 69 leads in 32 days

✅ $24 cost per lead with real job closes"

CTA:
"Comment 'ADS' below and I'll reach out"
```

**Pattern extracted:**
1. Specific dollar figure in hook
2. Anti-hustle reframe (effort isn't the lever, leverage is)
3. Three checkmark proof points (lead count, spend, CPL)
4. Comment-keyword CTA (engagement bait, IG-native)

**Critical for us:** the proof bullets work because they're SPECIFIC and CONSISTENT (lead count, spend, CPL all line up). We can't replicate Cesar's exact numbers. But we can replicate the FORMAT with verifiable numbers from our own clients once we have them.
