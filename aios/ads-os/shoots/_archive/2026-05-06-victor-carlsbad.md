## Coaching notes for talent

### For Peter (every script)

- Look directly at the lens, not at Dani.
- First line is the make-or-break. Re-shoot first line if energy drops.
- Don't smile in the hook. Smile after the hook lands.
- Sound like a person, not a marketer. If a line feels stiff, change the words, keep the meaning.
- Pace: slow on the pain points, faster on the offer, slow again on the guarantee.
- Hard rule: no em dashes in delivery. (You won't say one out loud anyway, but if you're reading from a printed script, the dashes are pause cues, nothing else.)

### For Victor (Script 3)

- Don't memorize. Read it once silently. Then say it your own way.
- It's OK to pause. It's OK to re-start. We'll cut.
- Look at Peter or look at lens, your call. Whichever feels natural.
- Your goal: sound like you're telling a friend at a barbecue what changed for you.
- Don't oversell. Understatement is more credible than hype here.
