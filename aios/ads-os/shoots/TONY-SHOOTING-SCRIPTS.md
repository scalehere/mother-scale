# CUT 1 — "Too Busy to Count"

**Format:** Conversational dialogue (mirrors Spark's 145-day longest-running ad)
**Length goal:** 15-20s in edit
**Talent:** Tony + Peter, both visible
**Setup:** Active pool site behind them. Rebar, tile work, equipment in frame. Lavs on chest, wind covers on.
**Track:** C — DM funnel
**Bible angle:** Story (MS31, MS35) | Hook #41

## How it goes

Peter prompts. Tony answers naturally. Peter pulls phone for receipts.

```
PETER: "Tony, how many leads we sent you last month?"
TONY: [downplay, hedge — "honestly bro, I lost count," "a lot," "more than I can take"]
PETER: [pulls phone, glances down] "I'm seeing about {actual number} in our log."
TONY: "Yeah, I'd have to look. We've been swamped."
PETER: [turns to lens] "See, this guy's too busy with jobs to count the leads. That's a good thing."
```

## Coaching for Tony
- Don't memorize. Just talk to Peter like you're at lunch.
- Hedging IS the proof. Don't try to give a confident number.
- "I don't remember" is gold. That's the whole point.
- Casual profanity OK if natural. Reads as real.

## Coaching for Peter
- Pull a real number from your phone (GHL log or text). Show your screen briefly if natural.
- Closing tag deadpan to lens. Half-smile after.
- You're the receipts-keeper, not the salesman.

## Body overlay (added in post)
- ✅ Real San Diego contractor | ✅ Booked through summer | ✅ 90 exclusive leads in 12 weeks

## CTA
"DM us 'SCALE'."

## Takes target
**3 takes minimum.**

# CUT 2 — "How Long, How Much"

**Format:** Conversational dialogue
**Length goal:** 20-30s
**Talent:** Tony + Peter
**Setup:** Pool site, possibly different angle (move so a finished section or completed deck is in frame)
**Track:** A — lead form
**Bible angle:** Story + Proof Stack (MS8, MS35)

## How it goes

```
PETER: "How long have you been working with us, Tony?"
TONY: [natural answer]
PETER: "How's the calendar looking now compared to before?"
TONY: [natural — fuller, picking jobs, etc.]
PETER: "How much new work has come through us, ballpark?"
TONY: [hedge, lowball — "a few jobs," "I'd have to check the books"]
PETER: [phone glance] "I think it was {figure}. We can pull the breakdown later."
TONY: "Could be. Either way I'm not complaining."
PETER: [to lens] "That's the difference. Real ads, real San Diego homeowners, real work in front of them."
```

## Numbers rule
Only say a dollar figure on camera if Dani and Ashen can verify from records that night. "Either way I'm not complaining" is the safety phrase if a number gets dropped in edit.

## CTA
"Tap below."

## Takes target
**3 takes.**

# CUT 3 — "Phone Before / Phone Now"

**Format:** Conversational dialogue (pain → contrast)
**Length goal:** 15-25s
**Talent:** Tony + Peter
**Setup:** Active site preferred (visual contrast to "phone working")
**Track:** A — lead form
**Bible angle:** Problem → Solution (MS25 paint success/failure)

## How it goes

```
PETER: "What was the phone like before we started?"
TONY: [natural — slow, off-season pain, referrals only, tire-kickers]
PETER: "What were you doing to fill it?"
TONY: [natural — referrals, neighbor word-of-mouth, hoping spring would save the year]
PETER: "And now?"
TONY: [natural — booked, picking which jobs to take, saying no to small ones]
PETER: [to lens] "That's what it looks like when the phone works for a contractor. If you want the same thing for your business, link's below."
```

## Coaching for Tony
- Be specific about the "before." Pain points sell. "I was wondering if I needed to lay off a guy" is gold.
- Don't sell. Just tell the story.

## CTA
"Tap below."

## Takes target
**2 takes.**

# CUT 4 — "Summer Locked Up"

**Format:** Tony solo monologue
**Length goal:** 15-20s in edit
**Talent:** Tony alone
**Setup:** In his truck OR sitting at the edge of a finished section. Phone-shot is fine. Peter off-camera.
**Track:** A — lead form
**Bible angle:** Identity + Founder Confession (MS18, MS31, MS6)

## Prompt before rolling
Peter says off-camera: "Tony, just tell the camera in your own words why any contractor in 2026 needs marketing. Don't sell us. Speak generally — to GCs, HVAC guys, roofers — not just guys in your trade."

## What we want him to say (his own words, NOT a script)
- Home service work is feast-or-famine. Summer is the year.
- He used to think marketing was for other people / a luxury / unnecessary
- Referrals weren't enough — they don't fire when you need them most
- Once Scale SD started running ads, he locked up summer by April
- He recommends it to other contractors now (any trade)

## The line we're hoping he lands
> "Times are changing. If you don't keep up, you're going to get left behind."

If Tony naturally arrives there or close, gold. Don't force it.

## Coaching
- Casual profanity is fine if natural. Reads as real.
- 4-5 takes minimum, each ~30-45 seconds. Edit will pull the strongest 15-20s line.
- No script. No memorizing.
- Address other trades, not just his own. "Contractors like me" not "pool builders like me."

## CTA (added in post overlay)
"San Diego contractors. Lock up your summer. DM 'SCALE'."

## Takes target
**4-5 takes minimum.**
