# Scale SD — Ideal Customer Profiles (the ICP multiplier)

> Each ICP is a separate persona. Same offer, different pain language, different triggers, different hooks.
> Use this file to multiply scripts: 1 angle x 7 ICPs = 7 scripts.

## Universal qualifiers (every ICP must hit these)

- San Diego County (or 30-mile radius)
- $200k+ annual revenue
- Owner-operated or owner + small team
- Already running their business at least 2 years
- Has work to show on camera (real projects, real job sites)
- Spends or has spent money on marketing before (not first-time experimenter)

## ICP 1 — General Contractor (GC)

| Attribute | Detail |
|---|---|
| Trade | General contracting, remodels, additions, full builds |
| Job size | $50k - $500k per job |
| Buying triggers | Just finished a big job and pipeline is empty. Coming out of slow season. Lost a referral source. Contractor friend got "more leads from ads." |
| Verbatim pain | "I'm done with one job and I don't know where the next one's coming from." "Referrals dried up." "I'm bidding on jobs I'm losing to guys with better marketing." |
| Verbatim pleasure | "Phone won't stop ringing." "Pre-booked 3 months out." "Picking which jobs I take." |
| Where they hang out | Facebook (industry groups), Instagram (project posts), TikTok (rare), local trade associations |
| Language | English primary. Some Spanish. |
| Hook angles that fit | guarantee, proof (Victor / VIP), pain (referral dependency), competition (their competitor's phone) |
| Avoid | technical CRM talk, complex funnel descriptions |
| Example client | Victor / VIP General Contractor |

## ICP 8 (excluded) — what we DON'T target

These verticals exist in home services but fail our qualifiers:

- House cleaning, car wash, mobile detail, junk removal — too low-ticket, can't afford retainer
- Solo handymen — under $200k revenue, no team
- Pure maintenance lawn-mowing operators — wrong job size
- Pre-revenue / first-year operators — no proof, no spend history

## How to use this file (for `ads-script-mass-generate` skill)

1. Pick a competitor ad's angle (e.g. "guarantee + speed-to-lead").
2. Pick an ICP (e.g. ICP 3 HVAC).
3. Multiply: rewrite the angle in HVAC pain language using HVAC verbatim phrases.
4. Run through `voice.md` register check.
5. Run through `writing-rules.md` slop filter.
6. Output goes into `creative-library/` with metadata: `{angle}_{icp}_{language}_{length}.md`.

7 ICPs x N angles per competitor x 2-3 length variants = the script multiplier.
