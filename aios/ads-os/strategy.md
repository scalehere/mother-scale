## Audience

**Primary:** San Diego home service contractors with $200k+ annual revenue. Established enough to afford retainer + ad spend. Want higher-quality clients, not bargain shoppers.

**High-priority verticals:**
- General contractors (remodels, full builds)
- Pool construction
- HVAC
- Roofing
- Plumbing
- Window/door installers
- Landscaping (high-ticket)

**Excluded:** cleaning, car washing, low-ticket home services (cannot afford retainer).

**Geo:** San Diego County. 30-mile radius from city center. North County coverage required (Carlsbad, Vista, Oceanside).

**Language split:** EN (Meta primary) + ES (TikTok primary, Rodrigo-style). Test both, double down on whichever produces higher-rev leads.

## Offer

**Core offer:** Contractor growth system. Local video creative + ad editing + paid media management + exclusive qualified leads.

**Guarantee (under final validation):** 90 qualified leads in 12 weeks. If we miss it, you don't pay.

**Anti-positioning:** Leads are NOT shared. Not Angi. Not Thumbtack. Not the same lead going to 4 other guys.

**Price points (not in ads, qualification call only):** retainer $1k-$2.5k/mo + ad spend.

## Differentiator

**The wedge:** Scale SD shoots locally. Real videographer, contractor's face, contractor's worksite, contractor's real projects in the ads. Not stock. Not banner ads. Not generic templates.

**The argument:** Modern contractors need to be on camera. Credibility and trust now drive lead quality. Banner ads and shared lead vendors are dead.

## Pain points to hit (in priority order)

1. Relying on referrals only. Inconsistent pipeline.
2. Buying shared leads from Angi / Thumbtack. Same lead went to 4 other guys.
3. Burning $2k+ on Google ads with two calls to show for it.
4. Posting on Facebook / Instagram and getting nothing.
5. Outdated banner ads. Invisible while competitors are on camera.
6. Feast-or-famine months. Slow seasons.
7. No system that produces predictable qualified opportunities.
8. "You're good at the work. You weren't trained in marketing."

## Funnel structure

### Track A — Lead Form (volume)
- Meta lead form, on-platform
- 3 qualifying questions
- Auto-routes to GHL pipeline → Dani's calendar
- Goal: max volume, qualify after on call

### Track B — Video Landing Page (quality)
- Velocity-style. Direct-to-camera pre-qualification video. Proof reel. Calendar booking.
- Pre-qualifies BEFORE the call so Dani only takes high-intent calls
- Higher CPL, higher show rate, higher close rate
- Built by Peter

**Strategy:** Run both in parallel. After 2 weeks of data, weight budget toward winner.

## Voice register

On-camera scripts use **Register C** (spoken). Plain English. Direct. Pain-first. No marketing jargon. Sound like a real person. Working-mind disfluencies OK ("look, the thing is...").

Static ad headlines use **Register A** (polished). Short. Direct. Benefit-first.

Hard rule across both: no em dashes.
