## Key tactics observed across the library

1. **Hedging is proof.** "I don't remember, maybe 200, 300k" sounds more credible than confident numbers.
2. **Cesar is the receipts-keeper, not the salesman.** Clients make the claims. Cesar fact-checks UP from his own records.
3. **Specific rapid-fire micro-numbers.** "$14K, $22K, $13K, $60K" creates accumulation effect.
4. **Truck / job site / outdoor** is the dominant setting. Office is rare.
5. **Casual profanity from clients** ("fucking huge," "no more fucking Yelp") increases authenticity.
6. **Comment "ADS"** is the dominant CTA in longest-runners. Not "click learn more."
7. **Five followers as proof.** Low social = real, not influencer. Counter-signal that Cesar uses repeatedly.
8. **No memorized scripts.** All testimonials sound conversational, hedging, restarting.
