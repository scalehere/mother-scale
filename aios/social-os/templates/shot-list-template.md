## 20-item shot list

### Pillar 1 — The Work (4-6 shots)

1. **Hero — completed project (wide):** {specific job, what to capture}
2. **Hero — detail/craftsmanship close-up:** {area / detail to highlight}
3. **Progress shot:** {if a job is mid-build}
4. **Branded truck/van in front of job site:** {vehicle ID}
5. **Before/after diptych:** {specific job — confirm "before" exists}
6. *(spare)* {extra Pillar 1 idea}

### Pillar 2 — The Team (2-3 shots)

7. **Owner portrait** (natural, on-site, holding tool or reviewing work)
8. **Full crew photo** (uniformed, at the job site)
9. **Action shot** (hands-on with tool, problem-solving)

### Pillar 3 — Education (2-3 shots)

10. **Close-up of common problem area** (with context for the tip post)
11. **Equipment / material being used** (for "what to look for" content)
12. **Seasonal tip visual** (current month — e.g., monsoon prep for May/June in SD)

### Pillar 4 — Social Proof (2 shots)

13. **Happy customer interaction** (with consent — handshake, walk-through)
14. **Screenshot / printed 5-star Google review** (graphic asset, can also be made later)

### Pillar 5 — Local Presence (1-2 shots)

15. **Job in recognizable SD neighborhood** (street sign / landmark visible without doxxing client)

### Video — Reels / TikTok (3-4 captures)

16. **15-30s before/after reveal** — highest-performing format for contractors
17. **30-60s day-in-the-life walkthrough**
18. **15s quick-tip direct-to-camera** (owner or lead, lavalier mic on)
19. *(spare)* {extra Reel idea — current trend / sound}

### Bonus — Offer/CTA (max 1/month)

20. **Promo graphic asset** (free estimate, seasonal special) — can be created in post if not on-site

## Shoot day schedule (2.5 hours)

| Time | Activity |
|------|----------|
| 0:00 – 0:15 | Arrive, walk location, confirm shot list with client |
| 0:15 – 0:45 | Hero shots — completed work (highest value, do first) |
| 0:45 – 1:15 | Team & action shots |
| 1:15 – 1:35 | Short-form video (Reel/TikTok) |
| 1:35 – 1:50 | Educational & detail shots |
| 1:50 – 2:10 | Local/misc/b-roll |
| 2:10 – 2:30 | Quick review with client; collect any client-supplied assets |
