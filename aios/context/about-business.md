## What the business does

A **marketing & automation agency** for contractors and local service businesses. The strategic positioning is **revenue partner** — not a marketing vendor. The pitch isn't *"we'll get you more leads,"* it's *"we'll help you close the leads you're already losing."*

**Core thesis:** most contractors miss calls, don't follow up on leads, and have no pipeline visibility. That's recoverable revenue. Scale SD fixes it with automation systems first, then layers content and paid ads on top.

## Strategic position

Mid-pivot from *traditional marketing agency* to *automation-first revenue partner*. SWOT (April 2026) confirmed:

- **Strengths:** full-stack capability under one roof, ideal-fit clients (contractors), fast/agile team
- **Weaknesses:** SOPs not standardized (custom-feeling delivery), team bandwidth as client count grows, two-brand confusion (Scale SD vs. ScaleHere), website still placeholder
- **Opportunities:** massive gap in contractor market, immediate-ROI sell, strong upsell path
- **Threats:** GHL agency saturation, scaling ahead of delivery capacity
