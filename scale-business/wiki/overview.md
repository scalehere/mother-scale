# Overview

**ScaleHere** (scalehere.com), operating as **Scale SD**, is a 5–6 person marketing and automation agency based in San Diego. The agency is mid-pivot from a traditional marketing agency to a **revenue partner** for contractors — selling measurable outcomes (jobs closed, revenue recovered) rather than marketing activity.

The central thesis: most contractors miss calls, don't follow up on leads, and have no pipeline visibility. This is recoverable revenue. Scale SD fixes it with automation systems, then layers in content and paid ads on top.

---

## Domain

**ScaleHere / Scale SD** — marketing and automation agency. Primary services: website/SEO/brand identity, social media content, paid ads, CRM automation (GoHighLevel). Platform: [[GoHighLevel]].

**Outbound target market:** contractors and local service businesses (roofing, HVAC, plumbing, remodeling, general contractors) — narrow funnel.
**Inbound:** open to any business that comes to Scale SD seeking services.

## Key Themes

- **Revenue partner over marketing vendor** — the positioning shift that unlocks higher ticket deals and better retention. See [[Revenue Partner Positioning]].
- **Automation as the core product** — the [[Contractor Automation System]] (missed call text-back, lead follow-up, booking, CRM, reviews) is the sticky, recurring-revenue engine.
- **Tiered service ladder** — Foundation ($1,800 one-time) → Growth ($1,200/mo) → Scale ($2,000/mo). Clear client journey. See [[Service Packages]].
- **Systems before scale** — the SWOT identifies that internal CRM, SOPs, and delivery systems must be dialed in before aggressive client acquisition.
- **Two-brand confusion** — Scale SD (contractors) vs ScaleHere (high-ticket broadly) is an unresolved weakness.
