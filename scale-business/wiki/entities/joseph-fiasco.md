# Joseph Fiasco

Owner and operator of [[EMSR (Emergency Mitigation Services Restoration)]], a restoration and remodeling contractor in La Mesa, CA. U.S. Army veteran. Signed a $2,500/month contract with [[Scale SD / ScaleHere]] on April 8, 2026.

## Role

Joseph is the primary client contact and sole decision-maker for the EMSR engagement — content direction, approvals, scope changes, and upsells all go through him.

## Communication Style

**Numbers-first, no fluff.** Joseph is data-driven and straightforward. Every conversation and report must be grounded in metrics and results. No jargon, no narrative padding. All strategy must be expressed in terms of the numbers it achieves and how. The framing he cares about: "Why are we doing X? What does it do to the cost per scheduled appointment?"

## Goals

- Cost per scheduled appointment: $250 or less (currently $456 minimum)
- Ad spend: scale to $40,000/month by end of 2026
- Not in survival mode — optimizing for quality, scalability, and brand authority

## Background Context

Previously had 84+ leads/month from other sources and turned them all down to restructure the business. The lead handling and tracking system was broken — leads were being generated but not captured, routed, or followed up on. This is the first thing Scale SD is fixing.

## Client Responsibilities (per agreement)

- Provide access to all accounts and media
- Share project locations for filming
- Respond to leads quickly
- Approve content when needed
- Send testimonials
