# Tad

Team member at [[Scale SD]]. Jointly responsible with [[Ashen]] for lead scraping and qualification, also owns external organic metrics analysis and co-leads AI/software training.

## Role
- Build and run scrapers for Google Maps and Yelp
- Manually filter and qualify leads (Sheet 1 → Sheet 2)
- Maintain shared lead list with mandatory notes per lead
- Deliver 150 qualified leads per week minimum
- **Organic Metrics (External)**: Research wide-variety data on video content and ads across the market — what patterns are working externally, not just internally
- **Software / AI Training** (joint with [[Justin]] and [[Ashen]]): Data collection, model training, and application logic for the AI ad pipeline

## Context
Tad's external metrics role complements Ashen's internal ads analytics — together they give Scale SD both a market-level and agency-level view of what content and ad formats are performing. This dual view feeds the AI ad creation pipeline and reduces guesswork on new ad creative.
