# GoHighLevel (GHL)

GoHighLevel is the core CRM and automation platform powering Scale SD's service delivery. It is the operational backbone for the agency's automation offer to contractors.

---

## Role at Scale SD

GHL powers:
- CRM and pipeline management for clients
- Missed call text-back automation
- Lead follow-up sequences (SMS + email)
- Appointment booking and calendar integration
- Review request automation
- The upcoming CRM Add-On product ($97/mo + $497 setup)

---

## Strategic Importance

The entire "Contractor Automation System" offer is built on GHL. Mastery of the platform is a core agency competency. One identified weakness in the SWOT: GHL systems are not yet fully dialed in (notifications, pipelines, lead tracking) — this needs to be resolved before aggressive client acquisition.

---

## Market Context

GoHighLevel has a large ecosystem of white-label agencies, which means the automation model is increasingly saturated. Scale SD's differentiation must come from execution quality and industry specialization (contractors), not the platform itself.
