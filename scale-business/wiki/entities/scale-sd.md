# Scale SD / ScaleHere

A marketing and automation agency based in San Diego. Operates under two brand names that serve slightly different markets:

- **Scale SD** — contractor-focused automation and marketing services
- **ScaleHere** (scalehere.com) — broader positioning for any high-ticket business

The two-brand structure is a current weakness — it creates confusion in the market and hasn't been fully differentiated in messaging or offer.

---

## Core Business

Scale SD positions itself as a **revenue partner** for contractors, not a marketing agency. The core promise: help contractors close more jobs by fixing the systems that lose them revenue — missed calls, no follow-up, no pipeline visibility.

Services span:
- Website development, SEO, and brand identity (Foundation)
- Content creation and social media management (Growth)
- Paid ads (Facebook & Instagram) + Local SEO retainer (Scale)
- CRM automation (GHL): missed call text-back, follow-ups, booking, reviews (Add-On)

## Client Acquisition Model

**Outbound:** Narrow funnel — targeted at contractors and local service businesses only (roofing, HVAC, plumbing, remodeling, general contractors).

**Inbound:** Open to any business that seeks Scale SD out. If a client comes to us, we work with them regardless of industry.

## Strategic Position

The agency is mid-pivot: transitioning from a traditional marketing agency to an automation/revenue partner. Key strategic shift — selling outcomes (revenue increase) rather than effort (marketing activity). This unlocks higher ticket clients ($3K–$5K/mo vs $1.5K/mo) and stronger retention.
