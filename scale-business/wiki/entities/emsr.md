## Strategy — Three Phases

See [[EMS Restorations General Strategy]] for full detail. Summary:

**Foundation**: Set up all accounts (Meta, IG, TikTok, GHL, website), fix lead tracking, remodel profiles, build organic content base, establish competitive intelligence. Complete when first ad goes live with all systems in place.

**Testing**: Systematic ad testing across creatives, audiences, landing pages, messaging. Connect APIs to track form fill → scheduled appointment. Optimize on actual appointments, not just form fills. Complete when cost per appointment hits $250 consistently.

**Acceleration**: Scale spend 15–20%/week maximum. Allocate 70–80% to proven creatives, 20–30% testing. Use cost caps to hold $250 ceiling. Expand audiences, geographies, project types.
