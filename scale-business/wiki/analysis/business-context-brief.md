## Strategic Position & Current Pivot

Scale SD is mid-pivot from a traditional marketing agency to an **automation-first revenue partner**. The SWOT (April 2026) identified:

**Strengths:**
- Full-stack capability — ads, content, automation under one roof
- Already working with ideal clients (contractors)
- Fast, agile team

**Weaknesses:**
- SOPs not yet standardized — delivery feels custom per client
- Team bandwidth is a constraint as client count grows
- Two-brand confusion (Scale SD vs ScaleHere)
- Website contains placeholder content — not yet updated to reflect real team, real clients, real case studies

**Opportunities:**
- Massive gap in contractor market — most miss calls, don't follow up
- Immediate ROI sell: "close more of the leads you already have"
- Service bundling creates strong upsell path

**Threats:**
- GHL agency saturation
- Scaling ahead of delivery capacity

**6-Month Roadmap (from internal strategy):**
- Months 1–2: Fix internal systems, close 3–5 contractor clients
- Months 3–4: Build case studies and testimonials
- Months 5–6: Launch agency's own paid ads, publish results, build referral system
