# Setter-Closer Sales Model

Scale SD's outbound sales system. Two roles, clearly divided: setters create and qualify conversations; closers convert them into paying clients.

---

## Why This Model

A single salesperson doing both outreach and closing burns out fast and stays inconsistent. By splitting the roles, Scale SD can:
- Run high-volume outreach daily without Daniel's attention
- Ensure Daniel (the closer) only spends time on qualified, ready-to-talk prospects
- Scale by adding more setters as the client base grows

---

## The Two Roles

### Setter
- Volume role — 50–100 messages/day, 10–20 conversations/day, 3–5 booked calls/day
- Platforms: Instagram, Facebook, Google Maps
- Does NOT pitch the service — opens with a pain-point question, qualifies, then books
- Currently: intern or junior team member

### Closer
- Quality role — 2–5 calls/day, 20–40% close rate
- Diagnoses the prospect's problem, amplifies the pain, pitches the system, closes
- Currently: Daniel

---

## The Outreach Script (What Makes It Work)

**Opening line:**
> "Hey — quick question, are you guys currently missing calls or just busy during the day?"

This works because it's a yes/no question about a real, felt problem. It doesn't lead with the agency or the service. When the contractor says "yeah, we miss calls sometimes," the setter has a qualified lead.

**Qualification question:**
> "Are you currently doing anything to follow up with missed leads or just handling everything manually?"

**Book the call:**
> "We've been helping contractors fix that and close more jobs from the leads they already have. Would it make sense to show you real quick how it works?"

---

## The Sales Call Framework (Closer)

1. **Intro** — disarm with curiosity, not a pitch
2. **Diagnose** — get them to tell you the problem in their own words
3. **Pain amplification** — quantify the lost revenue ("you're probably losing 10–15 opportunities monthly")
4. **Pitch** — one simple paragraph, not a deck
5. **Close** — frame it as a fit question, not a pressure question

See [[Objection Handling Guide — SD Contractors]] for how to handle pushback at any stage.

---

## Follow-Up System

Most deals close here — not on the first call.
- Day 1: simple follow-up message
- Day 2: case study + brief explanation
- Day 3: low-pressure close ("Still something you're open to or not right now?")

---

## Scale Path

Current state: Daniel is setter + closer (bottleneck).
Target state (5–10 clients):
1. Setter handles all outreach
2. Daniel only closes
3. After 10+ clients: hire a dedicated closer, Daniel moves to strategy
