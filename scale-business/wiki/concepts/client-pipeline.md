# Client Pipeline — Lead to Fulfillment

Scale SD's full operational pipeline from the moment a prospect enters the system to ongoing monthly delivery. The entire pipeline runs through [[GoHighLevel]].

---

## The 8-Stage Pipeline

```
LEAD IN → SALES CALL → PROPOSAL → CONTRACT → ONBOARDING → BUILD → LAUNCH → FULFILLMENT
Day 0      Day 1–3     Day 3–5    Day 5–7    Day 7–10    Day 10–18  Day 18–22  Day 22+
```

**Target:** Zero to live campaigns in 22 days. Zero to first month of fulfillment delivery within 30 days.

---

## Stage-by-Stage Summary

| Stage | Key Action | GHL Role |
|-------|-----------|----------|
| Lead In | Enter contact, auto sequences fire | Automation hub — SMS, email, rep notification |
| Sales Call | 30–45 min diagnosis call | Calendar booking, opportunity tracking |
| Proposal | Send via GHL within 24 hrs of call | Proposal tool, open tracking, follow-up automation |
| Contract | DocuSign via GHL, Stripe payment | Contract tool, payment collection, recurring billing |
| Onboarding | Welcome + intake form + kickoff call | Sub-account creation, status tracking |
| Build | Campaign build + tracking setup | Lead routing automations configured |
| Launch | Internal QA → client review → go live | Lead notifications activated |
| Fulfillment | Weekly optimization + monthly reporting | Ongoing pipeline updates |

---

## Non-Negotiables

- **Speed-to-contact:** First personal contact within 15 minutes of a new lead during business hours
- **Proposal timing:** Out within 24 hours of discovery call. Sitting 48+ hours = dramatically lower close rate
- **Tracking before launch:** Every conversion must show "Active" before any paid campaign goes live
- **GHL as single source of truth:** No leads tracked in spreadsheets, sticky notes, or personal phones

---

## How This Connects to Other Systems

The pipeline receives leads from the [[Setter-Closer Sales Model]] (outbound DM/call) and inbound sources (ads, referrals). Once a client is won, the [[Ads Onboarding & Fulfillment Process]] governs the technical build and ongoing delivery.
