A structured marketing planning framework that organizes strategy into five linked components: Goal, Strategy, Tactics, Implementation, and Controls. Plans flow top-down from objective to execution; evaluation flows bottom-up through controls. Used in the [[Strategic Marketing Management]] source as the master framework for all marketing plans.

## The Five Components

**Goal**
The specific objective the marketing plan is designed to achieve. Must be measurable and time-bound. Examples: increase market share by 10% in Q3, acquire 20 new contractor clients in 6 months, reduce cost-per-lead below $15.

**Strategy**
The market-facing choice: who to target, what to offer them, and how to position against alternatives. Strategy answers *where to compete* before tactics answer *how to compete*. A weak strategy makes excellent tactics irrelevant.

**Tactics**
The seven operational levers used to execute strategy (the 7Ps):
1. Product
2. Price
3. Place (Distribution)
4. Promotion
5. People
6. Process
7. Physical Evidence

For service businesses like [[Scale SD / ScaleHere]], People, Process, and Physical Evidence are the highest-leverage differentiators.

**Implementation**
The organizational and resource requirements: budget allocation, team roles, timelines, tooling, and incentive structures. A strategy with no implementation plan is a wish.

**Controls**
The metrics and feedback mechanisms that measure whether the plan is working. Controls close the loop — they catch drift early and trigger adjustments before small problems compound. Key metrics: CAC, CLTV, engagement rates, cost-per-lead, close rate.

- **Goal**: Specific client acquisition or revenue targets by month/quarter
- **Strategy**: Contractor-focused niche, revenue-partner positioning (outcomes not effort)
- **Tactics**: Outbound via [[Setter-Closer Sales Model]], paid ads, organic content, retargeting via [[Lead Warming System]]
- **Implementation**: Team role assignments (Daniel closes, Ashen/Tad source, Justin/Ashen build AI pipeline)
- **Controls**: Weekly lead counts, close rates, cost-per-lead on paid ads, client churn
