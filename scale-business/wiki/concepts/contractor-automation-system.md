# Contractor Automation System

The core productized offer Scale SD delivers to contractor clients. A bundle of automation systems built on [[GoHighLevel]] that addresses the specific revenue leakage problems contractors face.

---

## The 5 Core Components

### 1. Missed Call Text Back
Contractors miss calls constantly while working on-site. This system fires an automatic SMS to any missed caller, re-engaging them immediately with a conversational script that moves them toward booking.

### 2. Lead Follow-Up Automation
Multi-touch sequences via SMS + email:
- Immediate response on new lead
- Follow-up reminders (day 1, day 3, day 7)
- Reactivation campaigns for cold/stale leads

No lead is forgotten. Conversion rates improve significantly.

### 3. Appointment Booking System
Calendar integration with automated confirmations and reminders. Reduces no-shows. Shortens the sales cycle by removing friction from the scheduling step.

### 4. CRM & Pipeline Setup
Structured pipeline that tracks every lead from first contact to closed deal. Gives contractors full visibility into where they're losing jobs — which stage, which rep, which reason.

### 5. Review Automation
Triggered review requests after job completion. Builds Google and Yelp review volume consistently. Improves local SEO and inbound lead generation organically.

## Why This Works for Contractors

Most contractors:
- Miss calls while on the job site
- Never follow up on leads beyond the first attempt
- Have no structured sales pipeline
- Haven't asked for reviews systematically

Each of these is a solved problem with the right automation. The aggregate impact — more calls converted, more leads followed up, more jobs booked — represents recoverable revenue without any increase in ad spend.
