# Social Media Content Optimization Process

Source: `raw/SCALE SD STRATEGY DOCUMENTS/Social_Media_Content_Optimization_Process.docx`

Monthly content shoot + strategy and fulfillment SOP. One content specialist visits the client's job site for 2–3 hours per month. Everything needed for the full month of social media is captured in that window.

**Platforms:** Facebook, Instagram, Google Business Profile, TikTok (optional), Nextdoor (optional)
**Volume:** 12–16 pieces of content/month (3–4 posts/week)
**Client time required:** 2–3 hours for shoot + 30 minutes for content approval

---

## The 5 Content Pillars

Every piece of content maps to one of five pillars. Target monthly mix:

| Pillar | Theme | Monthly % | Approx Posts |
|--------|-------|-----------|--------------|
| 1 — The Work | Before/after, job-in-progress, finished installs | 35% | 4–6 |
| 2 — The Team | Owner/crew photos, behind-the-scenes | 20% | 2–3 |
| 3 — Education | Tips, how-to, warning signs, seasonal advice | 20% | 2–3 |
| 4 — Social Proof | Reviews, testimonials, job milestones | 15% | 2 |
| 5 — Local Presence | San Diego community, neighborhood callouts | 10% | 1–2 |
| Bonus — Offer/CTA | Promotions, free estimate offers | Max 1/month | 1 |

---

## Month 1 Strategy Session (New Clients Only)

45-minute session before first shoot. Defines:
1. Brand voice & personality
2. Content pillars tailored to this client
3. Target audience (homeowners in specific neighborhoods, age, income, pain points)
4. Platform priority (Facebook first for most contractors)
5. Competitor review — 2–3 local competitor profiles reviewed together
6. Offer & CTAs — what action should viewers take?
7. Shot list wish list — signature jobs, team, equipment, before/afters

---

## Shoot Prep (1 Week Before)

A poorly prepped shoot wastes the entire 2–3 hour window.

Key prep tasks:
- Review last month's top-performing posts
- Build shot list — minimum 20 shot ideas (capture 15–18, select 12–16)
- Confirm shoot location with client
- Send Pre-Shoot Brief to client (what to wear, what to have ready)
- Check upcoming SD events/holidays/seasonal hooks
- Charge all equipment

---

## 20-Item Shot List Framework

**Before/After (Pillar 1):** Wide shot of completed project; detail/craftsmanship close-up; progress shot; branded truck in front of job

**Team (Pillar 2):** Owner portrait (natural, on-site); full crew photo; action shot (hands-on); candid behind-the-scenes

**Education (Pillar 3):** Close-up of problem area with context; equipment/material being used; seasonal tip visual

**Social Proof (Pillar 4):** Happy customer interaction (with consent); screenshot of 5-star Google review; milestone graphic

**Local/Community (Pillar 5):** Job in recognizable SD neighborhood; community content

**Video — Reels/TikTok:** 15–30 sec before/after reveal (highest performer); 30–60 sec day-in-the-life walkthrough; 15-sec quick tip direct to camera

---

## Shoot Day Schedule (2.5 Hours)

| Time | Activity |
|------|----------|
| 0:00–0:15 | Arrive, walk location, confirm shot list |
| 0:15–0:45 | Hero shots — completed work (highest value, do first) |
| 0:45–1:15 | Team & action shots |
| 1:15–1:35 | Short-form video (Reel/TikTok) |
| 1:35–1:50 | Educational & detail shots |
| 1:50–2:10 | Local/misc/b-roll |
| 2:10–2:30 | Quick review with client, collect any client-supplied assets |

---

## Equipment Checklist

- DSLR or mirrorless camera (hero shots)
- iPhone 14 Pro or newer (vertical Reels)
- Phone gimbal/stabilizer (mandatory for video)
- Ring light or LED panel (interior/low-light)
- Wireless lavalier mic (talking-head video)
- Extra batteries and SD cards
- Shot list on phone
- Brand kit saved (colors, logos)

**Technical specs:** Photos minimum 2000px wide; video 1080p minimum for Reels.

---

## Key Claims
- Before/after reveal video is the highest-performing format for contractors
- Facebook is the primary platform for most contractor clients
- Max 1 promotional/CTA post per month to avoid audience fatigue
- Client involvement is capped at ~3 hours total — this is a key retention selling point
