# Scale SD — Setter & Closer Strategy

Source: `raw/SCALE SD STRATEGY DOCUMENTS/ Scale SD – Setter & Closer Strategy.docx`

Internal sales playbook defining the two-role outbound system. Goal: setters generate and qualify conversations; closers get on calls and close $1K–$3K+ monthly retainers.

---

## Team Structure

### Setter (Intern or Junior Team Member)
**Focus:** Outbound outreach, lead qualification, booking calls
**KPIs:**
- 50–100 messages/day
- 10–20 conversations/day
- 3–5 booked calls/day

### Closer (Daniel, for now)
**Focus:** Sales calls, diagnosing problems, closing deals
**KPIs:**
- 2–5 calls/day
- 20–40% close rate

---

## Target Market

Tight focus on contractor niches with high automation ROI:
- Roofing
- HVAC
- Plumbing
- Windows/Doors
- Remodeling

---

## Setter Outreach System

**Platforms:** Instagram, Facebook, Google Maps (manual). Optional later: SMS/cold email.

### Step 1: Find Leads
Search terms: "San Diego Roofing," "HVAC near me," "General contractor"
Look for: active business, has phone number, weak marketing/no systems.

### Step 2: First Message (NO PITCH)
> "Hey — quick question, are you guys currently missing calls or just busy during the day?"

Why it works: simple, hits a real pain point, doesn't feel like a sales pitch.

### Step 3: Qualify
> "Got it — are you currently doing anything to follow up with missed leads or just handling everything manually?"

Looking for: no system, missing leads, disorganized follow-up = perfect client.

### Step 4: Book the Call
> "Makes sense — we've been helping contractors fix that and close more jobs from the leads they already have. Would it make sense to show you real quick how it works?"

If yes → drop calendar link.

---

## Closer Call Framework (15–30 min)

### 1. Intro — Build Trust
> "Just want to learn a bit about your business and see if this actually makes sense for you."

### 2. Diagnose — Most Important Step
Ask:
- How many calls/leads per week?
- Do you miss calls?
- How do you follow up?
- How many jobs are you closing?

Goal: make them articulate the problem.

### 3. Pain Amplification
> "So if you're getting 20 leads and only closing 5… you're probably losing 10–15 opportunities monthly."

Make them feel the revenue being lost, not just acknowledge the problem.

### 4. Pitch — Simple, Not Complicated
> "What we do is install a system that: responds instantly to missed calls, follows up automatically, books appointments for you — so you stop losing those jobs."

### 5. Close
> "Honestly, this usually makes sense for contractors doing at least X leads per month — based on what you said, I think this could help you pretty quickly. Do you want help setting this up?"

## 3-Day Follow-Up Sequence

- **Day 1:** "Hey — just wanted to follow up, I think this could really help you recover lost jobs."
- **Day 2:** Send case study + simple explanation
- **Day 3:** "Still something you're open to or not right now?"

Most deals close in the follow-up, not on the first call.

---

## Daily Execution

| Role | Daily Actions |
|------|--------------|
| Setter | 100 messages, track in CRM, book 3–5 calls |
| Closer | Take calls, close deals, track objections |

## Scale Phase (After 5–10 Clients)
- Hire another setter
- Train a dedicated closer
- Build SOPs

## Key Claims
- Most agencies fail by trying to sell "marketing" and overcomplicating outreach
- Simple problem (missed leads) + simple message + clear ROI = the winning formula
- The setter system is volume-based; success requires consistent daily execution
- Closing is currently Daniel's responsibility — a bottleneck that resolves at 5–10 clients
