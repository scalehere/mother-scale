The signed marketing service agreement between Scale SD and EMSR (Emergency Mitigation Services Restoration, Inc.), effective April 8, 2026. Monthly retainer: $2,500. Covers social media content creation and management across a Facebook→TikTok→Instagram cascade model.

## Contract Terms

- **Effective Date**: April 8, 2026
- **Monthly Fee**: $2,500
- **Billing Cycle**: Monthly
- **Payment Terms**: Due on the 1st of each month; 5-day grace period; late fee applies after grace period

## Scope of Work

Scale SD provides social media content production and distribution under a cascade model:

1. **Facebook** — primary platform; all original content published here first
2. **TikTok** — repurposed/optimized content from Facebook
3. **Instagram** — repurposed/optimized content from Facebook

Content deliverables include: video production, caption writing, scheduling, and posting. The signed contract covers social media management only.

**Note on paid ads scope:** The operational engagement is broader than the signed contract. Per [[EMS Restorations General Strategy]], paid ads on Meta + TikTok are included in the engagement: $2,500/month covers content production and posting; ads management is billed at 8% of ad spend separately. The goal is to scale EMSR's ad spend to $40,000/month by end of 2026.

## Content Cascade Model

The Facebook-first cascade is a production efficiency strategy: create once, optimize for each platform's format, distribute across three channels. This multiplies content reach without proportionally multiplying production time.

Aligns with the [[Social Media Content Optimization Process]] SOP, which defines the monthly shoot-day schedule, caption framework, and platform-specific formatting guidelines.

## Legal Provisions

- **Ownership**: All content created under the agreement becomes client property upon full payment
- **Revisions**: Client entitled to a defined number of revision rounds per deliverable
- **Termination**: 30-day written notice required by either party
- **Confidentiality**: Both parties agree to non-disclosure of proprietary information
- **Liability Cap**: Scale SD liability limited to fees paid in the prior 30 days

## Context

EMSR is a restoration and emergency mitigation contractor — a service business targeting homeowners and property managers experiencing water damage, fire, or other emergency situations. This is a high-urgency, high-ticket niche where trust and credibility content (before/after, testimonials, rapid response) will be the primary content strategy.

At $2,500/month, EMSR is [[Scale SD / ScaleHere]]'s highest-value signed client to date (California Doors & Windows is at $2,000/month per the [[Client Proposals]] source).
