A practical outreach system walkthrough (via YouTube transcript) demonstrating a 2-step lead generation and SMS outreach system built inside GoHighLevel. Focuses on mobile-first targeting, high-volume SMS sequences, and power dialing to book calls. Directly applicable to Scale SD's outbound process.

## The 2-Step System

**Step 1 — Build a Targeted Lead List**
**Step 2 — Filter to highest-probability contacts, then outreach at scale via SMS + power dial**

## Step 1: Building the Lead List

### Free Tool
Chrome extension: extract business data directly from Google Maps. Search for a niche (roofers, landscapers, etc.), hit "Extract Business Data" — it auto-scrolls and exports to spreadsheet. Google caps at ~123 per search area. Workaround: zoom into sub-areas of the same city and re-run to compound the list.

### Paid Tool
**tryoutscraper.com** — Google Maps scraper. ~$12 for 4,000 businesses. Apply advanced filters:
- Has/doesn't have a website
- Verified Google profile
- Rating range (good or bad — both have use cases)
- Geographic radius

## Step 2: Qualifying the List

The single most important filter: **mobile phone numbers only**.

Why: mobile number = business owner's direct cell. Bypasses the front desk entirely. Most scraped lists mix mobile, fixed line, and landline. Sort by phone type, delete all non-mobile numbers.

**After filtering**: import to GoHighLevel via Contacts → Import.

## SMS Outreach Sequence

**Volume**: Start at 50 SMS/day, scale to 100/day. Multiple GHL sub-accounts (free to create) = 100/day per sub-account → potential 1,000+/day total.

**Opening message** (tested, high-performing):
> "Hey man, this is Pavel, looking you guys up on Google."

Short, human, non-salesy. Triggers curiosity without pitching.

**Expected results**: 2–4% positive reply rate is considered good.

**Follow-up sequence**: 3 automated follow-up messages. Non-responders → auto-tagged "Do Not Disturb" — never contacted again. Positive responders → notification triggers immediate outbound call.

## Pipeline Stages

```
SMS Sent → No Response → Follow-up 1 → Follow-up 2 → Follow-up 3 → DNC
                                                                        ↓
SMS Sent → Positive Reply → NOTIFICATION → Call ASAP → Book Demo Call
```

## Power Dialer

For calling at scale: select contacts in GHL → Trigger Automation → Power Dialer workflow → auto-dials one number after the next. Call outcomes logged (no answer, voicemail, interested, not interested, callback). Tags applied automatically at each stage.

**Golden Rule**: Do not have a full SMS conversation with prospects. Your only SMS goal after a positive reply is to get them on the phone. The only acceptable follow-up SMS: "Hey, I just tried to call you."

## On the Demo Call

- Show Google Business Profile gaps
- Show competitors (all have websites)
- Show your template/demo site (even with just their logo on it)
- Close to a paid engagement

## Key Claims

- Mobile-number filtering is the single highest-leverage qualifier — it determines whether you're reaching the owner or a gatekeeping front desk
- 2–4% positive reply rate on SMS is realistic and sufficient at scale
- The power dialer removes all manual dialing friction — you just assess each call and hit next
- Sub-accounts in GHL are free, so outreach volume scales linearly with sub-account count
- Speed to lead matters: positive replies should be called within minutes, not hours
