# Lead Generation and Qualification System

Source: `raw/Lead Generation and Qualification System (1).pdf`

Written by Daniel in an Amazon-style layered format: Unfiltered Thinking → Organized Thoughts → Finalized System → Tasks of Establishment. Documents the thinking, logic, and final system for building the lead pipeline. This format is intended to be adopted as a team-wide process for system development going forward.

---

## The Core Problem

The main growth bottleneck is call volume. The calling window is **11am–1pm, Tuesday through Saturday** (2 hours/day, 10 hours/week). Everything outside those hours must prepare for those hours. No system = slow or negative growth.

Two constraints: **Time** (fixed) and **Leads** (needs to be solved). Preparation — lead list building with notes — happens in the non-calling hours.

---

## What Qualifies a Lead

A lead who has shown *some* effort toward building an online presence but isn't there yet. If a contractor has 3K+ Instagram followers and a strong website, they don't need Scale SD. The target: businesses that have tried but failed to execute consistently.

---

## The Finalized System (Two-Sheet Structure)

### Scraping Sources
Google Maps and Yelp.

### Sheet 1 — Scraped Leads (Unfiltered)
Every lead pulled from any scraping source goes here.

**Columns:** Business name, phone number, point of online presence (URL), scrape source (Google Maps or Yelp), city/location.

**First pass filter:** Remove any lead missing a phone number or a point of online presence. Delete from Sheet 1.

### Manual Analysis (Second Pass)
For every remaining lead, open all online presence points and evaluate:

| Channel | Disqualifiers (mark red, do not call) | Qualifiers (passes to Sheet 2) |
|---------|--------------------------------------|-------------------------------|
| Website | Strong, conversion-optimized, social proof | Outdated, poorly designed, missing, no CTA, no portfolio |
| Instagram | 3K+ followers, consistent, professional content | Posted in last 90 days but inconsistent; low quality; bad brand; no keyword SEO |
| Facebook | Consistent, professional | Inconsistent; posted in groups to find business (signals effort) |
| Google Business Profile | Complete, verified, strong reviews | Missing info, low reviews, unclaimed, no posts, low-quality photos |
| Yelp | Strong reviews, professional photos | Low reviews, bad images, sparse/unmanaged |
| TikTok | — | Has account, tried posting in last 90 days |

**Rule:** If ALL channels are strong → mark red, skip. If ANY single channel is weak, incomplete, or missing → qualifies, move to Sheet 2.

### Sheet 2 — Qualified Leads
**Columns:** Business name, phone number, city/location, mandatory notes, website, Instagram, Facebook, Google Business, Yelp.

**Notes are mandatory** for every lead. Note quality directly impacts call quality.

Notes must include:
1. What is weak or missing across their online presence
2. What they have clearly attempted in the past (running ads, having a content person, posting consistently at some point)
3. Anything that stands out as a direct conversation starter or sales angle for the call

## The Target Number

**150 qualified leads per week.** 30/day. 15 calls/hour across the calling window. This is the starting baseline.

## Key Claims
- Calling window is 10 hours/week — this is the constraint the entire lead gen system is built around
- In-person meetings outperform phone meetings — this is noted but deprioritized until call volume is solved
- Note quality on leads is treated as a direct driver of call conversion quality
- The system is explicitly designed to have Ashen and Tad maintain a lead buffer so Daniel never runs out of qualified contacts to call
