## Client 1 — California Doors & Windows

**Contact:** Michael Marohnic
**Agreement Date:** December 1, 2025 (signed by Daniel J Loarca)
**Monthly Fee:** $2,000/month
**Status:** Active client (signed)

**Services included:**
- Content creation, production, editing, planning
- Marketing strategy, full overview, content calendar
- Social media management: Instagram, Facebook, YouTube
- CTA development
- Marketing planning and optimization
- High-quality photo and video editing
- Community manager / multichannel interaction
- 2–3 graphic design photos + 10 Reels/month
- 3–4 posts/week
- Monthly in-person 4-hour filming session

**Add-on available:** Paid advertising (Facebook/Instagram) — $1,000/month if added

**Client problem addressed:** California Doors & Windows had no active social media presence. Competitors were already using Instagram, Facebook, and TikTok. The pitch: showcase premium products (vinyl/composite windows, interior/exterior doors, sunrooms, patio covers, walk-in tubs, remodeling), build credibility, and drive new business leads through visual storytelling.

**Payment terms:** Full payment due upfront. 2-week cancellation notice. 5% monthly interest on late payments. Content remains Scale SD property until paid in full.

## Client 2 — VIP General Contractor

**Contact:** Victor Pilar
