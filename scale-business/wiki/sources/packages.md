# Service Packages

Source: `raw/Packages.pdf`

This document captures both the legacy package structure and the redesigned, cleaner package suite Scale SD is moving toward.

---

## Current / Redesigned Package Suite (Active)

These are the 4 packages Scale SD is building toward — a cleaner, tiered structure with clear customer journey logic (Foundation → Growth → Scale).

### 1. Foundation — $1,800 (One-time)
"Start here. A professional home base that turns visitors into calls."
- Custom conversion-focused website
- Google Business Profile setup & optimization
- Yelp Business Profile setup
- Full SEO setup + keyword research
- Full brand identity + logo suite

### 2. Growth — $1,200/month (3-month minimum)
"Real content from real job sites — posts, Reels, and strategy that builds trust and creates ad-ready assets."
- Instagram, Facebook + TikTok or LinkedIn
- 3–5 posts/week + 7 Reels/month
- Monthly content shoot (3 hours on-site)
- Content calendar & full strategy
- Monthly report + strategy call

### 3. Scale — $2,000/month (3-month minimum · Foundation required) ⭐ Recommended
"Content, ads, and SEO all running together. This is when the phone doesn't stop ringing."
- Everything in Growth
- Facebook & Instagram paid ads
- Local SEO retainer
- Dedicated account manager
- Bi-weekly strategy calls

### 4. CRM System Add-On — $97/month + $497 setup (Coming Soon)
AI-powered GHL CRM system. Full CRM, automated follow-ups, missed call text-back, review requests. Add to any plan, cancel anytime.

## Legacy Package Structure (Prior)

| Product | Price | Billing |
|---------|-------|---------|
| Foundation Custom Digital Presence Package | $2,000 | One-time |
| Social Media Marketing \| Growth Package | $2,000 | One-time |
| Organic Meta Full-Service Digital Marketing | $1,235 | Monthly |
| Full-Service Digital Marketing (FB & IG) | $1,200 | Monthly |

The legacy packages were less differentiated and priced higher for the one-time offers. The redesign sharpens the value ladder and lowers Foundation entry price ($1,800 vs $2,000).

---

## Key Claims
- Packages follow a logical customer journey: Foundation first, then add Growth, then unlock Scale
- Scale package requires Foundation — creates natural upsell and ensures client has a proper digital base
- CRM add-on is the automation play, tied to GoHighLevel — currently "coming soon"
- 3-month minimums on recurring packages protect agency revenue and allow time to show results
