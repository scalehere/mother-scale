# EMS Restorations General Strategy

Internal strategy document prepared by Scale SD for the [[EMSR (Emergency Mitigation Services Restoration)]] account. Covers the client overview for the ads team, a full marketing growth plan, execution timeline, and unfiltered strategic thinking. The source went through multiple drafts (raw thinking → organized concepts → finalized outline → growth plan → execution checklist).

## Key Claims

- **The core problem**: EMSR does not have a demand problem — it has a lead handling, tracking, and conversion problem. 84+ leads/month were previously coming in and being lost before Scale SD onboarded.
- **Current cost per scheduled appointment**: minimum $456 (8 clicks × $57/click at general construction CPC rates).
- **Target cost per scheduled appointment**: $250 or less. This is the single KPI the entire ads engagement is measured against.
- **Ad spend trajectory**: Scale to $40,000/month by end of 2026.
- **Peak season**: January through end of June. Signed April 9, leaving ~6–8 weeks of peak season. Speed of execution is the highest priority variable.
- **Pricing**: $2,500/month flat for content production + posting. 8% of ad spend for ads management (mentioned in raw notes).
- **Joseph is a numbers guy**: every client-facing communication must be grounded in metrics and results, no jargon or fluff.
- **Website/landing pages**: hosted on Joseph's privately managed WordPress/Elementor server. Scale SD designs and delivers assets; Joseph controls hosting.
- **CRM**: GoHighLevel. Every lead must trigger an immediate SMS + email to Joseph the moment it comes in. GHL setup flagged as highest-priority task.
- **Bot filtering**: a priority as spend scales — forms must filter bots before leads enter the pipeline.

## Business Overview

EMSR operates two connected business lines:

**Emergency damage restoration** — water, fire, mold, biohazard. High-urgency, high-intent, non-price-shopping customers. Speed and trust are the decision factors. Over 1,000 insurance claims handled. License #1081134, website: emsrestorations.com. 24/7 availability.

**Remodeling and reconstruction** — kitchens, bathrooms, full reconstructions, home additions, ADU builds, multi-family/HOA work. Planned purchases; customers compare contractors over days or weeks and care about quality and proof of work.

Both lines feed each other: emergency customers can become remodeling clients; remodeling clients call first in emergencies. Average restoration job: $10,000–$100,000. Average remodeling project: $10,000–$30,000. Single converted lead = high revenue, so quality over volume is the mandate.

## The Three-Phase Strategy

### Phase 1: Foundation
Complete all one-time setup before any meaningful ad spend:
- Gain access to all accounts: Facebook Business Page, Meta Ads, Instagram, TikTok, website, Docusketch (media library)
- Create and configure GHL: lead form, email/SMS notifications, tested end-to-end lead flow — **highest priority**
- Profile remodel: graphics, story covers, bios on all platforms
- Media audit: review existing files, identify gaps, build media needs list, schedule filming
- Build two GHL-integrated landing pages (per contract)
- Set up competitive intelligence monitoring (Meta + TikTok ads and organic, local + national)
- Begin cyclical organic posting: Day 1 → Facebook, Day 2 → Instagram, Day 3 → TikTok, repeat. Stories 3–5x/week. Also post to Google Business Profile.
- Foundation is complete when first ad goes live with all tracking systems in place.

**Note on competitive research**: Month 1 = manual competitor analysis; automate after baseline is established.

### Phase 2: Testing
- Launch ads on Meta + TikTok using best-performing organic content as creative
- Connect APIs to track the full funnel: form fill → scheduled appointment (not just form fills)
- Optimization layer 1: minimize cost per lead form fill
- Optimization layer 2: shift to cost per scheduled appointment as the optimization target
- Systematically test: landing pages, forms, audiences, messaging types, creative formats
- Allocate budget continuously toward best-performing creatives; cut what isn't working
- Hone brand voice, retargeting strategy, ad spend allocation through iteration
- Testing is complete when cost per scheduled appointment hits $250 consistently.

### Phase 3: Acceleration
- Increase ad spend max 15–20% per week (to avoid resetting Meta's learning phase)
- Budget split: 70–80% proven creatives/audiences, 20–30% continuous testing
- Introduce retargeting campaigns and lookalike audiences
- Use cost caps to prevent cost per appointment from drifting above $250 at scale
- Rotate and refresh creatives to fight ad fatigue at higher spend levels
- Expand horizontally into new audiences, geographies, project types
- Monitor lead quality continuously — bot volume increases with spend; tighter forms + audience filtering are the primary tools

## Two Operational Systems

Scale SD identified two distinct systems to run simultaneously:
1. **Intelligence & Analysis Loop** — competitor monitoring, internal analytics, organic metrics → informs what to produce next
2. **Execution System** — media gathering, content production, posting, ads management → acts on the intelligence
