A reference document containing 100 contractor-focused social media content hook ideas, organized into 8 thematic buckets. Designed to give Scale SD and its clients a ready supply of proven content angles that drive engagement and sales for local service businesses.

## Content Hook Buckets

**1. Before & After / Transformation**
Visual proof-of-work content. Showcases completed jobs to establish quality and credibility. Examples: dramatic bathroom renovations, flooring installs, exterior repaints.

**2. Process / Behind the Scenes**
Shows the work being done — timelapse builds, tool walkthroughs, crew in action. Builds trust by demonstrating professionalism and craftsmanship.

**3. Educational / Tips**
Positions the contractor as the local expert. Examples: "3 signs your roof needs replacing," "what to ask before hiring a contractor," maintenance tips by season.

**4. Social Proof / Testimonials**
Client reviews, on-camera testimonials, star rating callouts, thank-you messages. Offsets skepticism and handles the "trust" objection passively.

**5. Problem / Solution**
Hooks that call out a specific pain point and present the contractor as the fix. Examples: "Is your driveway cracking? Here's why — and how we fix it."

**6. Urgency / Seasonal**
Time-bound content tied to weather, seasons, or events. Examples: "Winter is coming — is your HVAC ready?" or post-storm emergency response messaging.

**7. Personality / Culture**
Team spotlights, company story, values, humor, and behind-the-brand content. Builds familiarity and likability — reduces the "stranger" barrier to hiring.

**8. Offer / Call to Action**
Direct promotional content: free estimates, limited-time discounts, referral programs, new service launches. Converts engaged viewers into leads.

## Strategic Logic

The 100 hooks span the full awareness-to-conversion arc:
- **Top of funnel**: Educational, Behind-the-Scenes, Personality
- **Middle of funnel**: Before/After, Problem/Solution, Social Proof
- **Bottom of funnel**: Urgency, Offer/CTA

This mirrors the [[Contractor Automation System]]'s philosophy: organic content warms leads passively, reducing the cost and friction of paid conversion. The structure also aligns with the [[Lead Warming System]] — organic engagement contributes to warmth scores when tracked.

## Key Claims

- 100 hooks provide enough variety to run a full year of content without repetition
- Contractor-specific framing (vs. generic social media advice) increases relevance and client value
- The bucket structure maps naturally to a monthly content calendar (see [[Social Media Content Optimization Process]])
- Educational and transformation content consistently outperforms direct-offer content in organic reach for service businesses
