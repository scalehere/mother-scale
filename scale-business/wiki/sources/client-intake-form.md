A standardized onboarding intake form template used by Scale SD to collect all necessary business and access credentials from new clients at the start of an engagement. The form covers identity, service scope, platform access, and review setup.

## Form Sections

**Section 1 — Business Information**
Core business details: business name, owner name, phone, email, website, physical address, and business hours.

**Section 2 — Service Package Selection**
Client indicates which services they are receiving: Social Media Management, Paid Ads, SEO, Lead Generation, or other (custom). This records the contracted scope for fulfillment.

**Section 3 — Social Media Access**
Captures login credentials or admin access for all active platforms: Facebook, Instagram, TikTok, LinkedIn, and any other platforms in use.

**Section 4 — Google Business Profile (GBP) Setup**
- Business name as listed on Google
- Google account email associated with GBP
- Verification status (verified / not verified)
- Request for admin access to the GBP listing

**Section 5 — Yelp Profile Setup**
- Yelp business page URL
- Yelp account login credentials
- Notes on review status or existing responses

**Section 6 — Additional Notes**
Open field for anything the client wants to communicate — past marketing efforts, special instructions, goals, or context not covered above.

## Operational Role

This form is the entry point for all client onboarding within [[Scale SD / ScaleHere]]'s delivery pipeline. It feeds directly into the [[Client Pipeline (Lead to Fulfillment)]] — specifically the Onboarding stage — and provides the credentials and context needed to begin ad setup, social media management, and CRM configuration in [[GoHighLevel]].

## Key Claims

- Standardized intake reduces onboarding friction and ensures no credentials or platform details are missed
- GBP and Yelp sections indicate Scale SD actively manages local SEO and review reputation as part of service delivery
- The "additional notes" field allows custom context that may affect fulfillment strategy
