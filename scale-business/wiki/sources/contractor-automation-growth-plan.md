# Contractor Automation Agency: 6-Month Growth Plan

Source: `raw/SCALE SD STRATEGY DOCUMENTS/Contractor_Automation_Growth_Plan_v2.pdf`

Executive overview of how to build and scale a contractor-focused automation agency. Core thesis: help contractors increase revenue by capturing missed opportunities and automating follow-ups — without relying on additional lead generation spend.

---

## Positioning & Offer

Position as a **revenue partner**, not a marketing service provider. Core promise: help contractors close more jobs using automation systems. ROI is immediate and measurable — this makes it easier to sell than vague marketing outcomes.

---

## Core Automation Systems (The Product)

### 1. Missed Call Text Back
- Contractors miss calls constantly while on-site
- System auto-sends SMS to missed callers, re-engaging them instantly
- Scripts guide the lead toward booking an appointment

### 2. Lead Follow-Up Automation
- Multi-touch sequences via SMS + email
- Covers: immediate response, reminders, reactivation campaigns
- Ensures no lead falls through the cracks

### 3. Appointment Booking System
- Scheduling system integrated with contractor calendars
- Automated confirmations + reminders reduce no-shows
- Shortens sales cycle, increases operational efficiency

### 4. CRM & Pipeline Setup
- Structured pipeline: initial contact → closed deal
- Gives contractors visibility into where they're losing opportunities
- Foundation for measuring and improving the sales process

### 5. Review Automation
- Automated review requests triggered after job completion
- Improves local SEO rankings and inbound lead flow
- Builds long-term brand authority and credibility

---

## Pricing Strategy

Hybrid model: setup fee + monthly retainer. Optional performance-based bonuses tied to closed deals. This structure aligns agency incentives with client success and improves retention.

See [[Service Packages]] for current productized pricing.

---

## Target Market

High-value contractor niches with high ticket sizes and high rates of missed opportunities:
- Roofing
- HVAC
- Plumbing
- General contractors / remodeling

These industries benefit most from automation because one recovered job frequently exceeds the cost of the entire monthly retainer.

## 6-Month Growth Plan

| Phase | Months | Focus |
|-------|--------|-------|
| Acquire | 1–2 | Initial clients via outreach and networking |
| Deliver | 3–4 | Results delivery, case study building |
| Scale | 5–6 | Referrals, content marketing, paid ads |

---

## Scaling Systems

To grow without sacrificing quality:
- Hire support staff
- Create SOPs for all service delivery
- Standardize onboarding and fulfillment processes

---

## Key Claims
- Automation agencies can differentiate in a crowded market by focusing on revenue outcomes, not marketing activity
- Contractors are the ideal niche: underserved by tech, high-ticket jobs, clear automation ROI
- The missed call problem alone represents significant recoverable revenue for most contractors
