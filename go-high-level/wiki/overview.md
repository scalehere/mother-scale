## Core Model: GHL as an Agency Platform

GHL has two layers:
- **Agency view** — manages clients, SaaS plans, usage rebilling
- **Subaccount view** — where the actual work happens (CRM, automations, websites, messaging)

Three agency revenue streams: client retainers + usage rebilling markup + white-label SaaS reselling (497 plan only).

## Automation System: Three-Tier Learning Ladder

The central architecture across all automation sources:

```
1. Basic Builder          →  single trigger, linear actions, learn the mental model
2. Advanced Builder       →  drag-and-drop canvas, multiple triggers, keyboard shortcuts (Labs)
3. AI Builder             →  natural language → full automation + copy in seconds
```

**Critical principle:** Learn in order. AI amplifies what you know — it cannot substitute foundational understanding. This is the single most repeated insight across sources.

**Automations are meta-tools:** They automate other GHL features (contacts, calendars, pipelines, payments, messaging). Learn those features first, then automate them.

## Emerging Themes

1. **Speed of contact matters most** — Fast Five and missed call text-back both optimize for the critical first minutes after a lead enters.
2. **Social media automations are underrated** — GHL can replace ManyChat for comment → DM flows natively.
3. **AI is a force multiplier, not a shortcut** — consistent across all 6 sources.
4. **Field mapping is a common failure point** — social lead form data lost when platform fields don't match GHL fields.
5. **Rebilling and SaaS mode are the path to agency scale** — but require the $497/month plan.

## AI Builder — Updated Picture (from Official Docs)

The AI builder is more capable than the video sources indicated:
- **3 entry points** (not 2)
- **<30 seconds** generation (fast)
- **Autosave** on all AI edits
- **Point and Edit** — click steps, describe change, AI edits only those steps (great for 10+ action workflows)
- **Chat Mode** — plan without building; brainstorm first, then generate
- **Conversational editing** of If/Else branches and Wait steps

## Workflow Chaining (from Official Docs)

A key advanced pattern missing from video sources: one workflow per pipeline stage, each one removing the contact from the previous when advancing. Enables clean, modular multi-stage automation without one giant complex workflow.
