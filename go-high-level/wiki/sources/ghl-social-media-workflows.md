## Summary

Covers four GHL automation strategies for social media growth and lead generation: (1) Facebook/Instagram comment → DM automation using keyword triggers, (2) social lead form automation handling all four ad platforms (Facebook, Instagram, TikTok, LinkedIn), (3) recurring automated Google review post to social media, (4) custom social automation mixing and matching triggers/actions.

## Key Takeaways

- **Comment → DM automation:** Trigger fires when someone comments a specific keyword on a post (e.g., "haircut"). Automation replies to the comment, likes the comment (for engagement boost), then sends a DM — all automatically. Toggle "send only once per contact" to avoid repeat sends.
- **Keyword specificity matters:** Running it on all comments risks sending to irrelevant commenters. A specific phrase targets intent and lets you call-to-action in content ("comment HAIRCUT for a coupon").
- **AI chatbot handoff:** After the DM is sent, enable GHL's AI chatbot on the channel to handle the full conversation — fully automating the sales process.
- **Social form lead gen:** Facebook, Instagram, TikTok, LinkedIn lead form submissions can all be triggers — four platforms in one workflow. Requires field mapping (Facebook fields ↔ GHL fields) in Settings → Integrations. Common beginner mistake: mismatched field names = lost data.
- **Recurring review post:** In Marketing → Social Planner → "Post Reviews" — pulls 5-star Google/Facebook reviews and posts them to social accounts (feed + stories) on a schedule. Set once, runs forever. Takes ~2 minutes to configure.
- **Form mapping warning:** Always ensure lead form fields from the ad platform exactly match GHL contact fields. Mismatch = data not captured correctly.
- **Agency selling angle:** Comment automation and review posting can be sold to clients as social media management — value is obvious and setup is ~2 minutes.
