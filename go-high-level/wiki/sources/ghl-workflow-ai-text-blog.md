## Summary

Short official GHL blog post announcing and explaining the Workflow AI text-to-workflow feature. Confirms two entry points, highlights that AI handles branching/conditions, auto-generates copy, and can make smart assumptions (e.g., reply-based triggers, dynamic values).

## Key Takeaways

- **Two entry points:** (1) Inside Workflow Builder when creating a new workflow — AI prompt box is present. (2) Automations tab → "Build with AI" button.
- **Suggested automations:** The AI prompt box also offers clickable suggested automations (pre-written prompts) you can select with one click.
- **Complex prompts work:** Example: "Create a 3-email and SMS follow-up flow for webinar leads. Branch based on whether they're new or existing contacts, tag them and move them into the pipeline." — AI maps triggers, branching paths, emails, texts (fully written), wait steps, pipeline movement.
- **Smart assumptions:** AI applies reply-based triggers and dynamic values where appropriate — not just a literal translation of the prompt.
- **Auto-generated copy:** Emails and SMS written as part of generation.
- **Inline refinement:** After generation, refine the results directly from the AI interface.
