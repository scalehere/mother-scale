# GoHighLevel (GHL)

An all-in-one marketing and CRM platform aimed at marketing agencies and small businesses. Combines CRM, email/SMS marketing, funnel building, website hosting, reputation management, and workflow automation in a single platform. Notable for its white-label capability — agencies can rebrand and resell it.

## Key Facts

- **Business model:** SaaS subscription; agencies resell under their own brand (white-label)
- **Core differentiator:** Everything an agency needs in one platform — replaces ClickFunnels, ActiveCampaign, Calendly, ManyChat, and others
- **Automation engine:** Three-tier builder system — Basic, Advanced (drag-and-drop canvas), and AI-powered
- **Target market:** Digital marketing agencies, freelancers selling automation services, small-to-medium businesses
- **Pricing tiers:** $97/month (basic), $297/month (pro), $497/month (unlimited + SaaS Configurator)

## Platform Structure

Two layers:
- **[[GHL Agency View]]** — admin layer for managing all client subaccounts, SaaS plans, rebilling
- **[[GHL Subaccount View]]** — the business/client layer where automations, CRM, and marketing live

## Revenue Model for Agencies

Three income streams when reselling GHL:
1. **Retainer fees** — fixed monthly charge for managing the client's GHL subaccount
2. **[[GHL Rebilling]]** — small markup on usage costs (SMS, email, WhatsApp, AI minutes)
3. **SaaS reselling** — white-label GHL as your own SaaS product via [[GHL SaaS Configurator]] (497 plan only)

## Automation Architecture

GHL's automation system has three layers:
1. **[[GHL Automation Builder (Basic)]]** — linear, single-trigger workflows
2. **[[GHL Advanced Automation Builder]]** — drag-and-drop canvas, multiple triggers, keyboard shortcuts (available via Labs)
3. **[[GHL AI Automation Builder]]** — natural language → full automation generation including copy
