# GHL Workflow Recipes

Pre-built workflow templates built into [[GoHighLevel]]. Recommended starting point for beginners — start with a recipe and edit it rather than building from scratch.

## How to Access

When creating a new workflow: Create Workflow → select **"Recipes"** (one of three options alongside "Start from Scratch" and "Select from Template").

## What They Are

Recipes are GHL-curated workflow structures for the most common automation use cases. They come with triggers, actions, and sometimes copy pre-configured. You customize them for your specific business.

## Why Use Them

- Saves time vs. building from scratch
- Reveals what automations GHL considers most valuable/common
- Good learning tool — inspect a recipe to understand structure before building your own

## Relationship to AI Builder

The [[GHL AI Automation Builder]] generates custom workflows from prompts and is distinct from Recipes (which are static pre-built templates). For common patterns, recipes may be faster than writing an AI prompt. For custom/complex flows, AI builder is more flexible.

## Relationships

- [[GHL Automation Builder (Basic)]] — accessed within the workflow creation flow
- [[GHL AI Automation Builder]] — alternative creation method
