# Fast Five Follow-Up

An automation pattern for immediately hammering a new lead with 5 touchpoints the moment they enter the CRM. Referenced in the 4-hour course as one of the most important lead conversion automations.

## How It Works

As soon as a trigger fires (lead form submitted, contact created, etc.):
1. Immediate touchpoint 1 (typically SMS)
2. Immediate touchpoint 2 (email)
3. Short wait
4. Touchpoint 3 (call or voicemail drop)
5. Touchpoint 4 (SMS follow-up)
6. Touchpoint 5 (final outreach attempt)

The exact sequence and channels vary by business — the principle is **speed and persistence at lead entry**. Most leads go cold within minutes if not contacted.

## Why It Works

The biggest problem in lead gen is not the leads — it's the follow-up gap. People who pay for ads and get leads often have no automated follow-up. Fast Five closes this gap instantly.
