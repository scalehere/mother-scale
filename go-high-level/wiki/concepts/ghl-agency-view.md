# GHL Agency View

The top-level administrative layer in [[GoHighLevel]], used by the agency owner to manage all client subaccounts, configure SaaS plans, and view consolidated analytics. Distinct from [[GHL Subaccount View]] (the client/business layer).

## Key Capabilities

- **Agency Dashboard:** Consolidated MRR, total revenue, new/total clients, growth charts
- **[[GHL SaaS Configurator]]:** Auto-provision subaccounts from paid SaaS plans (497 plan only)
- **Rebilling:** Configure usage markup (SMS, email, WhatsApp, AI) per subaccount
- **Subaccount management:** Create, search, pin, transfer, duplicate across subaccounts
- **Prospecting tool:** Find local businesses (costs extra; creator suggests ChatGPT as free alternative)
- **Account switcher:** Switch between agency view and any subaccount; search + pin for quick access

## Plan Tiers

- **$97/month:** Basic — subaccount view only, no SaaS Configurator
- **$297/month:** Pro — more subaccounts
- **$497/month:** Unlimited + SaaS Configurator + advanced reselling

## Relationships

- [[GHL Subaccount View]] — the business layer it creates/manages
- [[GHL SaaS Configurator]] — plan management and auto-provisioning
- [[GHL Rebilling]] — usage markup configuration
- [[GHL Snapshots]] — templates deployed to new subaccounts
