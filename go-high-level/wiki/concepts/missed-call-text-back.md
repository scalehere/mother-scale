# Missed Call Text-Back

One of the most commonly recommended GHL automation recipes. When an inbound call is missed, an SMS is automatically sent to the caller within seconds.

## How It Works

- **Trigger:** Call status = missed (inbound)
- **Action:** Send SMS — e.g., "Hey! Sorry we missed your call. How can we help you today?"

## Why It Matters

Most missed calls = lost leads. An immediate text-back re-engages the caller before they call a competitor. Businesses that deploy this report significant lead recovery.

**Agency selling angle:** Easy to demo, immediately impactful, clients understand the value instantly. One of the best "wow" automations to show a prospect.

## Variations

- **WhatsApp version:** Missed call → WhatsApp message (covered in 4-hour course as "Missed call WhatsApp text-back")
- **AI version:** Missed call → AI chatbot initiates the conversation
